# PSALM 66 — Come and Hear What He Did for Me

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 66:1–20. Starts with the whole earth, ends with one man's testimony.*

---

This psalm zooms in. It starts with the planet and ends with one guy saying *let me tell you what happened to me.*

**"Shout for joy to God, all the earth! Sing the glory of His name! Make His praise glorious!"**

*All the earth.* Widest possible lens. Every continent.

**"Say to God: 'How awesome are Your deeds! Through the greatness of Your power Your enemies cringe before You. All the earth bows to You. They sing to You. They sing to Your name.'"**

*Awesome.* There's *nora* — the *yirah* word. Awe-inspiring. Jaw-dropping. Not "terrible." The whole earth isn't cowering — it's *singing*.

Now — the invitation, and it comes twice in this psalm with a different object each time:

**"Come and see the works of God. His deeds toward the children of Adam are awesome."**

*Come and see.* First invitation. *Look at what He did.*

"He turned the sea into dry land. They crossed the river on foot. There — we rejoiced in Him."

*There — we rejoiced.* The Exodus. The Jordan. He's saying *we* — as if he was there. Because in the memory of Israel, everybody was there. Every generation crossed on dry ground. Your story includes the rescues you didn't personally witness.

**"He rules by His might forever. His eyes watch the nations. Don't let the rebels exalt themselves."**

Now — the hard part. And it's honest:

**"Bless our God, you peoples. Let the sound of His praise be heard. Because You've tested us, God. You refined us like silver.**
**You brought us into the net. You laid a heavy load on our backs. You let men ride over our heads. We went through fire and through water —**
**and You brought us out into abundance."**

Read the sequence. *Tested. Refined. Net. Heavy load. Men riding over our heads. Fire. Water.* That's not one bad thing — it's *seven*. And every one has *You* in front of it. *You* brought us into the net. *You* laid the load.

He's not blaming God to complain. He's crediting God for the *whole* arc — because if God's in the net, God's also in the exit. *And You brought us out into abundance.* The Hebrew is *r'vayah* — overflow, saturation. Psalm 23's *cup runs over*.

Through fire and water — to overflow. That's the route. There isn't a shortcut around the fire.

**"I'll come into Your house with burnt offerings. I'll pay You my vows — the ones my lips spoke and my mouth promised when I was in trouble."**

*When I was in trouble.* You know the prayer. *God, if You get me out of this, I'll —*. He's here to pay it. The bargain from the foxhole gets honored in the temple.

And now — the zoom. From all the earth, to the people, to *one man*:

**"Come and hear — all you who stand in awe of God — and I'll tell you what He did for my soul."**

*Come and hear.* Second invitation. First was *come and see* the works of God. Now it's *come and hear* what He did *for me*.

*For my soul.* Not for the nation. Not for history. *Mine.* He's got a personal story and he wants an audience.

**"I cried to Him with my mouth. Praise was on my tongue."**

*Cried — and praised.* Both, at once. Same mouth. The plea and the thanks in the same breath — because he'd already decided how it would end.

**"If I'd held wrongdoing in my heart — the Master wouldn't have listened."**

He checked. Psalm 26 again — *test me*. He did the inventory before he prayed.

**"But God HAS listened. He paid attention to the voice of my prayer."**

*Has.* Done. Past tense. He's not hoping. He's *reporting*.

**"Blessed be God — who has not turned away my prayer, or His loyal-love from me."**

*Not turned away my prayer or His loyal-love.* Two things He didn't remove. The prayer got through. The love stayed put.

Twenty verses. Here's the shape: *All the earth — shout. Come see what He did in history. He put us through fire and water and brought us to overflow. I made a promise in the foxhole and I'm here to pay it. Now — come here, close — let me tell you what He did for ME.*

Your testimony isn't a footnote to the big story. It's the *zoom*. The place where all the earth's praise gets a face. Tell it. *Come and hear.*
