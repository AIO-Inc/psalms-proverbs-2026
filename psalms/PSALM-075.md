# PSALM 75 — I Choose the Time

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 75:1–10. Asaph. God's answer to "how long?" — and it's not a date.*

---

Psalm 74 asked *how long* and got no prophet to answer. Psalm 75 is God answering.

**"We thank You, God — we thank You. Your name is near. People tell of Your wonderful deeds."**

*Your name is near.* After the ash of 74 — *near*. Not "back." *Near.* He never left the neighborhood.

And then — God speaks. First person. And the first thing He says answers the *how long*:

**"'When I choose the appointed time — I will judge with fairness.'"**

*When I choose.* Not when you ask. Not when the enemy's done. *When I choose the time.* The Hebrew is *mo'ed* — a fixed, appointed moment. Like a festival on the calendar. It's *scheduled*. You don't know the date. He does.

That's the answer to *how long*. Not a number. *I've got the calendar.*

**"'When the earth and all its people quake — it is I who hold its pillars firm.'"**

*I hold the pillars.* The ground's shaking — the temple's rubble — and God says: *the reason it hasn't ALL fallen is Me. I'm holding the posts.* The shaking isn't evidence He let go. The fact anything's still standing is evidence He didn't.

**"'To the arrogant I say: Boast no more. To the wicked: Do not lift up your horns. Do not lift your horns against heaven. Do not speak with an outstretched neck.'"**

*Horns.* A bull lifting its head to charge. Pride with a *posture* — chin up, neck out, ready to hit. God's saying: *put it down.*

Now — Asaph again, and this is the theology:

**"No one from the east or the west or from the desert can exalt themselves."**

You can't *promote yourself*. Not from any direction. The arrogant think they climbed. They didn't.

**"It is God who judges. He brings one down — He exalts another."**

*Brings down. Exalts.* Two hands. Same God. Nobody's up because they're clever. Nobody's down because they're cursed. The ups and downs are *administered*.

Hannah sang this — 1 Samuel 2. Mary sang it — Luke 1. *He has brought down rulers from their thrones and lifted up the humble.* Psalm 75 is where they got it.

And then — the cup:

**"In the hand of the Source is a cup — full of foaming wine, mixed with spices. He pours it out. All the wicked of the earth drink it down — to the very dregs."**

*A cup.* Psalm 60 — *wine that makes us stagger*. Jeremiah 25 — the cup of wrath passed to the nations. And in Gethsemane — *Father, if it's possible, let this cup pass from Me.*

*To the dregs.* The sediment at the bottom. Every drop. The wicked don't get to sip and set it down. They finish it.

Here's what to hold: the cup exists. Justice isn't a fairy tale. Psalm 73's wicked man with the bulging eyes and the fans — there's a cup with his name on it, and God's hand is on it, and the time is *appointed*. You don't have to pour it. You don't have to make him drink. You just have to know it's *real*.

**"But as for me — I will declare this forever. I will sing praise to the God of Jacob."**

*But as for me.* Asaph's version of David's *but I*. *They* drink the cup. *I* sing.

And the last line — God's voice again, or Asaph speaking for Him:

**"'I will cut off the horns of all the wicked — but the horns of the righteous will be lifted up.'"**

*Horns cut off. Horns lifted up.* The bull that charged heaven gets dehorned. The one who kept his head down gets *raised*.

Ten verses. Here's the answer to *how long*: *I choose the time. I'm holding the pillars. Put your horns down. Nobody climbs on their own — I bring down and I lift up. There's a cup, and it's full, and they'll finish it. The horns of the proud come off. The horns of the humble go up.*

You don't know the date. That's fine. It's on *His* calendar — and He's never missed an appointment.
