# PSALM 32 — When I Kept Silent

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 32:1–11. David's confession after Bathsheba — the "after" to Psalm 51's "during." Augustine had this one written on the wall by his bed.*

---

This is the psalm about what happens when you finally tell the truth.

**"Happy is the one whose rebellion is lifted off — whose missing-the-mark is covered.**
**Happy is the one to whom the Source doesn't count wrongdoing — in whose spirit there's no deceit."**

Three words for what went wrong — *rebellion*, *missing the mark*, *wrongdoing*. Three words for what God does — *lifted off*, *covered*, *doesn't count*. He's stacking them up because one word wasn't enough for either side.

And the last phrase: *in whose spirit there's no deceit*. That's the condition. Not "who never did it." *Who stopped lying about it.*

Now the story — and every addict, every person who's carried a secret, knows this part cold:

**"When I kept silent, my bones wore out — from my groaning all day long. Day and night Your hand was heavy on me. My strength dried up like summer heat."**

*When I kept silent.* That's the whole problem in three words. He didn't confess. He held it. And his *body* paid. Bones wearing out. Strength evaporating. Groaning he couldn't stop. The secret was eating him from the inside, and it wasn't God punishing him — it was the *weight* of the unsaid thing.

Every person who's ever hidden something knows: the hiding costs more than the thing.

Then — the turn. And it's one verse:

**"I acknowledged my missing-the-mark to You. I stopped covering my wrongdoing. I said: 'I will confess my rebellions to the Source.' And You — You lifted the guilt of my missing-the-mark."**

Watch the sequence. *I said I would confess.* And before he even finishes — *You lifted it.* Not after a probation period. Not after he'd made it up. The moment he *decided to say it*, the weight came off. Verse 1 said "covered." Verse 5 says he "stopped covering." That's the trade: *you stop covering it, and He covers it.* You can't both cover the same thing. One of you has to let go.

"So let every loyal one pray to You while You can be found. When the great waters flood — they won't reach him."

*While You can be found.* There's a window. Not because God moves — because *you* do. The longer you keep silent, the harder it is to speak. Confess while it's still one thing, before it's a life.

**"You are my hiding place. You guard me from trouble. You surround me with shouts of rescue."**

*Hiding place.* Here's the irony — he spent verses 3–4 hiding *from* God, and it was killing him. Now he's hiding *in* God, and it's saving him. Same instinct — hide — pointed in the opposite direction. That's the whole turn: stop hiding from Him, start hiding in Him.

Now God speaks — direct, first person:

**"I will instruct you and teach you the way you should go. I will counsel you — with My eye on you."**

*My eye on you.* Not surveillance. A parent watching a kid learn to ride a bike. Ready to step in. Present. *Watching you the way you watch someone you love.*

"Don't be like the horse or the mule — no understanding — that has to be controlled with bit and bridle or it won't come near you."

*Don't make Me use the bridle.* You can come the easy way — you hear, you turn. Or you come the hard way — bones wearing out, strength drying up, until you finally give in. Both roads end in the same place. One of them hurts a lot more.

**"Many are the sorrows of the mark-misser. But loyal-love surrounds the one who trusts in the Source."**

*Surrounds.* Same word as verse 7 — *shouts of rescue surround me.* When you come clean, you're not just forgiven. You're *encircled*. Loyal-love on every side.

**"Be glad in the Source and rejoice, you who do right! Shout for joy — all you upright in heart!"**

*Upright in heart.* Not "clean-record." *Upright* — pointed straight, facing the right way. That's David after Bathsheba. He's not pretending it didn't happen. He's standing up straight anyway, because the weight's off.

So: what are you keeping silent? It's in your bones already. Say it. He's already got the cover ready — He's just waiting for you to take yours off.

*Note on translation: v. 1 —* nasui *= lifted off, carried away (same root as Ps 25:18);* kasui *= covered. v. 3 —* hecherashti *= I kept silent/deaf. v. 5 —* lo chisiti *= I did not cover — deliberate contrast with v. 1. v. 8 — direct divine speech;* iy'atzah alecha eini *= "I will counsel with My eye upon you." v. 9 — the bit-and-bridle image is about unnecessary force, not God's preference. No* yirah*.*
