# PSALM 96 — Sing a New Song

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 96:1–13. An enthronement psalm — "the Source reigns" — aimed at the whole earth. Every tree gets a part.*

---

Psalm 93 said *the Source reigns*. Psalm 96 says — *so tell everybody. And get the trees involved.*

**"Sing to the Source a new song! Sing to the Source, all the earth!"**

*A new song.* Psalm 33, 40 — fresh material. Not because the old songs are bad. Because *He did something new* and it needs its own melody.

*All the earth.* Not Israel. *All.* Every continent's invited to the choir.

**"Sing to the Source. Praise His name. Proclaim His salvation day after day."**

*Day after day.* Not once. A *rhythm*. Psalm 61 — *day by day I pay my vows*.

**"Declare His glory among the nations — His marvelous deeds among all peoples."**

*Among the nations.* You don't sing this one inside the temple with the doors closed. You take it *out*. To the people who've never heard the name.

**"Because great is the Source — and most worthy of praise. He is to be held in awe above all gods."**

*Awe.* *Yirah* — *nora*. Above all gods. Not "feared." *Awe-inspiring beyond every rival.* The word the KJV rendered "feared" here, in the middle of a verse commanding *praise*.

**"Because all the gods of the nations are idols — but the Source made the heavens."**

*Idols.* The Hebrew is *elilim* — a pun. *Elohim* means gods. *Elilim* means *nothings*. Worthless things. The nations' gods are *elilim* — *nothing-gods*. And the Source *made the sky they're looking at*.

**"Splendor and majesty are before Him. Strength and glory are in His sanctuary."**

Now — the invitation to the nations. And it's *specific*:

**"Ascribe to the Source, all you families of nations — ascribe to the Source the glory due His name."**

*Families of nations.* Not governments. *Families.* Genesis 12 — *all the families of the earth will be blessed.* The invitation goes to the *household* level.

**"Bring an offering and come into His courts. Worship the Source in the splendor of His holiness."**

*Come into His courts.* The foreigners — *inside*. Psalm 87 — *born in Zion*. The door's open to the families that used to be enemies.

**"Tremble before Him, all the earth!"**

*Tremble.* The Hebrew is *chilu* — writhe, shake, be moved. Psalm 2 — *rejoice with trembling*. It's not terror. It's the *physical response* to standing in front of something that big. Your knees do it on their own.

And now — the three words:

**"Say among the nations: 'The Source reigns!'"**

*Say it among the nations.* Psalm 93's three words — carried *out*. To Babylon. To Egypt. To the places that have their own kings. *Yours isn't the real one. Here's Who is.*

**"The world is firmly established. It cannot be moved. He will judge the peoples with equity."**

*Cannot be moved.* Psalm 93 — *the world is established*. Psalm 15, 16, 21, 30 — *not shaken*. Because the King's on the throne.

*Equity.* Fairness. Psalm 82's failed gods tilted the scale. The real King judges *straight*.

And then — the whole creation joins in. Watch how it *spreads*:

**"Let the heavens rejoice! Let the earth be glad! Let the sea resound — and all that is in it! Let the fields be jubilant — and everything in them!"**

Heavens. Earth. Sea. Fields. Everything *in* them. The fish. The wheat. The whole thing *singing*.

**"Let all the trees of the forest sing for joy!"**

*The trees.* Every one. Psalm 92's palm and cedar. Psalm 1's tree by the water. Psalm 52's olive. Now the *whole forest* — every trunk and branch — *singing*. Isaiah 55 — *the trees of the field will clap their hands.* Paul in Romans 8 — *creation groans, waiting*. Psalm 96 says: *and then it SINGS.*

**"Let them sing before the Source — because He comes — He comes to judge the earth."**

*He comes.* Twice. *He's coming.* Not to condemn — to *judge*. To *set things right*. To fix Psalm 82's courts. And the trees are *glad about it* — because when the Judge comes, the ones who've been crushed get lifted, and the creation that's been groaning gets *heard*.

**"He will judge the world in right-standing — and the peoples in His faithfulness."**

*Faithfulness.* The last word. The Judge is *faithful*. He doesn't rule by mood. He rules by *promise*. Psalm 89's seven *emunah*s — here's where they land.

Thirteen verses. Here's the shape: *New song — all the earth. Day after day. Among the NATIONS. He's awesome above the nothing-gods. Families — come in. Tremble. Say it out there: the Source REIGNS. The world can't be moved. Heavens, earth, sea, fields — sing. TREES — sing. He's coming to set it right. Faithfully.*

Tell somebody who doesn't know. Then go outside and listen to the trees. They already do.

*Note on translation: v. 1 —* shir chadash *= new song (cf. 33:3, 40:3). v. 4 —* nora hu al-kol-elohim *=* yirah *= "awesome [is] He above all gods" — KJV "to be feared." v. 5 —* elilim *= idols/worthless things — pun on* elohim*. v. 7 —* mishp'chot amim *= families of peoples (Gen 12:3, 28:14). v. 9 —* chilu mi-panav *= tremble/writhe before Him. v. 10 —* YHWH malach *= "YHWH reigns" (cf. 93:1). v. 12 —* kol-atzei-ya'ar *= all trees of the forest. v. 13 —* ki va ki va *= "for He comes, for He comes." Nearly identical to 1 Chronicles 16:23–33.*
