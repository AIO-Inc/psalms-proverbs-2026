# PSALM 87 — This One Was Born There

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 87:1–7. Sons of Korah. Seven verses. The most radical psalm about who gets to belong — and it's about a birth certificate.*

---

Seven verses. And they rewrite who counts as *from here*.

**"He has founded His city on the holy mountain. The Source loves the gates of Zion — more than all the dwellings of Jacob."**

*Loves the gates.* Not the temple. Not the palace. The *gates* — the entrances. The place where people *come in*. God's favorite part of the city is the door.

**"Glorious things are said of you — city of God."**

Then — the roll call. And it's not who you'd expect:

**"'I will record Rahab and Babylon among those who acknowledge Me —**
**Philistia too — and Tyre — along with Cush —**
**and will say: This one was born in Zion.'"**

Read the list. *Rahab* — that's Egypt, the old slavemaster. *Babylon* — the empire that burned the temple (Psalm 74, 79). *Philistia* — Goliath's people. *Tyre* — the trading power. *Cush* — Ethiopia, the far south, the end of the map.

The enemies. The oppressors. The foreigners. The *far away*.

And God says: *I'm writing them down. In the book. And when I write their name — I'll say: "This one was born in Zion."*

*Born in Zion.* A *birth certificate*. Not "naturalized." Not "granted residency." *Born here.* The Egyptian, the Babylonian, the Philistine — God's *rewriting their origin*. Not where they came from — where they *belong*.

That's the most radical thing in the Psalms. Book Two and Three kept saying *the nations will come*. Psalm 87 says *they were never foreigners. I'm changing the paperwork.*

**"Indeed — of Zion it will be said: 'This one and that one were born in her.' And the Most High Himself will establish her."**

*This one and that one.* God pointing. At the Egyptian — *born here*. At the Babylonian — *born here*. One by one. Personal.

**"The Source will write in the register of the peoples: 'This one was born in Zion.'"**

*The register.* The census. The official record. God as the clerk — writing *Zion* in the "place of birth" field for people who were born in Babylon.

Paul spent Ephesians 2 on this. *You who were far away — fellow citizens.* Hebrews 12 — *you have come to Mount Zion.* Revelation 21 — *the nations will walk by its light.* Psalm 87 got there first.

And the last verse — the party:

**"As they make music they will sing: 'All my fountains are in you.'"**

*All my fountains.* Every spring. Every source of life. Psalm 36 — *the fountain of life*. Psalm 46 — *the river that makes glad the city*. The Babylonian, the Philistine, the Cushite — *singing* that every fountain they've got is in Zion. Not back home. *Here.*

Seven verses. Here's the shape: *He loves the gates. Glorious things are said. Egypt — born here. Babylon — born here. Philistia, Tyre, Cush — born here. This one and that one. He's writing it in the book. And they're singing: all my fountains are in You.*

Here's what to do with it: whoever you've decided is *from somewhere else* — the enemy, the outsider, the one whose passport says the wrong thing — God's got a pen, and He's writing *Zion* in their birthplace field. You don't get to argue with the register.

And if *you're* the one who thinks you're from somewhere else — check the book. He might've already written it.
