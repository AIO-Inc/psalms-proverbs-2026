# PSALM 147 — He Heals the Brokenhearted and Counts the Stars

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 147:1–20. Twenty verses. Second of the five closing Hallelujahs. Post-exile — Jerusalem's being rebuilt, the exiles are coming home. The psalm swings between the biggest thing God does (naming stars) and the smallest (binding a wound) — and refuses to see a difference.*

---

Psalm 146 — *don't trust princes; here's who God shows up for*. Psalm 147 — *here's what it looks like when He does*. And it starts with a city getting its walls back.

**"Hallelujah! How good it is to sing praises to our God — how pleasant and fitting to praise Him!"**

*Good. Pleasant. Fitting.* *Tov. Na'im. Na'vah.* Psalm 133 — *how good and how pleasant*. Psalm 147 — *same two words* — and adds *fitting*. Praise isn't just nice. It *fits*. Like a key in a lock. It's what the mouth is *for*.

**"The Source builds up Jerusalem. He gathers the exiles of Israel."**

*Builds up Jerusalem.* *Boneh Y'rushalayim YHWH.* Present tense. *Is building.* Nehemiah's walls. Ezra's Temple. Psalm 137 — *by the rivers of Babylon*. Psalm 147 — *coming HOME*. The harps come out of the trees.

*Gathers the exiles.* *Nidchei Yisrael y'kannes.* The *scattered ones* — *nidchei* — the *pushed-out*. Deuteronomy 30 — *He will gather you from all the nations*. Psalm 147 — *doing it*.

Now — the pairing that makes this psalm what it is:

**"He heals the brokenhearted — and binds up their wounds. He determines the number of the stars — and calls them each by name."**

Read those two verses together. *Brokenhearted. Stars.*

*Heals the brokenhearted.* *Ha-rofe li-sh'vurei lev.* The *shattered* of heart. Psalm 34 — *near to the brokenhearted*. Psalm 51 — *a broken spirit*. Psalm 147 — *HEALS*. Not just near. *Rofe.* The *doctor* word. Exodus 15 — *I am the Source who heals you*.

*Binds up their wounds.* *M'chabbesh l'atz'votam.* *Bandages* their *griefs*. *Atz'vot* — sorrows, hurts. He wraps them. Physically. Like a medic. Isaiah 61 — *bind up the brokenhearted* — the passage Jesus read in Nazareth. Psalm 147 said it first.

*And* — same breath — *He counts the stars and names every one.*

*Moneh mispar la-kochavim.* Genesis 15 — *count the stars, if you can*. Abraham couldn't. Isaiah 40 — *He brings out the starry host by number, calls each by name*. Psalm 147 — *He knows how many. He knows their NAMES.*

Here's the point. The One who names *every star* — billions upon billions — *also* wraps the wound on *your* heart. Same hands. Same afternoon. He doesn't do the big thing *instead* of the small thing. He doesn't have to choose. The star-counter is the medic. If He's got time for every star — He's got time for your break.

**"Great is our Master and mighty in power. His understanding has no limit."**

*No limit.* *Ein mispar.* *No NUMBER.* He *numbers* the stars. His understanding *has no number*. The Counter can't be counted.

**"The Source sustains the humble — but casts the wicked to the ground."**

*Sustains the humble.* *M'oded anavim.* *Lifts* the lowly. Psalm 145, 146 — *lifts the bowed down*. Psalm 147 — *anavim* — the *humble*. The ones who don't lift themselves.

Now — a *second* round. Praise — then reasons. The psalm's got three cycles. Here's the second:

**"Sing to the Source with grateful praise. Make music to our God on the harp. He covers the sky with clouds. He supplies the earth with rain — and makes grass grow on the hills. He provides food for the cattle — and for the young ravens when they call."**

*Grass on the hills.* Psalm 104 — *grass for the cattle*. Psalm 147 — *He GROWS it*. The rain doesn't just fall. It's *supplied*. *Ha-mechin.* *Prepared.*

*The young ravens when they call.* *Li-v'nei orev asher yikra'u.* The *raven chicks*. Job 38 — *who provides food for the raven when its young cry out?* Psalm 147 — *He does*. Luke 12 — *consider the ravens. They don't sow or reap — God feeds them.* Jesus picked the same bird. The ugliest, loudest, least lovable bird in the Bible — Leviticus calls it *unclean* — and its *babies cry* — and God *feeds them*.

If He hears the raven chick — He hears you.

**"His pleasure is not in the strength of the horse — nor His delight in the legs of the warrior. The Source delights in those who stand in awe of Him — who put their hope in His loyal-love."**

*Not the horse. Not the legs.* Psalm 20 — *some trust in chariots, some in horses*. Psalm 33 — *a horse is a vain hope*. Psalm 147 — *He's not IMPRESSED*. Not by the war-horse. Not by the *legs* — *shokei ha-ish* — the runner's thighs. The athlete. The soldier. The strong.

*He delights in those who stand in awe of Him.* *Rotzeh YHWH et-y're'av.* *Yirah.* Not *fear*. *Awe.* God's *delight* — *ratzon* — Psalm 145's word — is in the *awe-people*. Not the strong. The *awed*. The ones who look at the stars and *know* they didn't hang them.

*Who hope in His loyal-love.* *Ha-m'yachalim l'chasdo.* Psalm 130 — *I wait for the Source*. Psalm 147 — *waiting on chesed*. That's what awe looks like when it sits down — it *waits*.

Third cycle:

**"Extol the Source, Jerusalem. Praise your God, Zion. He strengthens the bars of your gates — and blesses your people within you. He grants peace to your borders — and satisfies you with the finest of wheat."**

*The bars of your gates.* *B'richei sh'arayich.* Nehemiah 3 — *they set up its doors, its bolts and its bars*. Psalm 147 — *He STRENGTHENS them*. The city has *gates* again. Psalm 122 — *our feet are standing in your gates*. Psalm 147 — *and the gates LOCK now*.

*Finest of wheat.* *Chelev chittim.* The *fat* of the wheat. Psalm 81 — *with honey from the rock I would satisfy you*. Psalm 147 — *the best flour*. After the exile's rations — *the fat of the wheat*.

**"He sends His command to the earth. His word runs swiftly. He spreads the snow like wool — and scatters the frost like ashes. He hurls down His hail like pebbles. Who can withstand His icy blast? He sends His word and melts them. He stirs up His breezes — and the waters flow."**

*His word runs swiftly.* *Ad-m'herah yarutz d'varo.* *His word RUNS.* Psalm 19 — *nothing is hidden from the sun's heat*. Psalm 147 — *the word sprints*. Isaiah 55 — *My word will not return to Me empty*. It goes out — *fast*.

*Snow like wool. Frost like ashes. Hail like pebbles.* Three pictures. Soft, fine, hard. All *His*. Job 38 — *have you entered the storehouses of the snow?* Psalm 147 — *He spreads it*. Like a blanket. *Wool.*

*He sends His word and melts them.* *Yishlach d'varo v'yamsem.* The same *word* that froze it — *thaws* it. Winter and spring — *one word*. And the waters *flow*. The frozen thing *moves* again. If you've got a frozen place — *He sends His word*. Same word. Different season.

And the last three verses — the *word* again, but now it's a *gift*:

**"He has revealed His word to Jacob — His laws and decrees to Israel. He has done this for no other nation. They do not know His laws. Hallelujah!"**

*Revealed His word to Jacob.* *Maggid d'varav l'Ya'akov.* The word that *runs* across the earth — *stops* at Jacob and *speaks*. Psalm 119 — the eight words for the Word. Psalm 147 — *given to Jacob*. Deuteronomy 4 — *what other nation has laws like these?*

*For no other nation.* Not *boasting*. *Responsibility.* Psalm 67 — *that all nations may know*. Psalm 117 — *praise Him, all nations*. Psalm 147 — *you got the Word first. Now they need to hear it.* Romans 3 — *what advantage has the Jew? Much — they were entrusted with the words of God*. Entrusted. To *carry*.

Twenty verses. Here's the shape: *Hallelujah — it FITS. He's rebuilding Jerusalem. Gathering the scattered. Healing the brokenhearted — and NAMING THE STARS. Same hands. No limit. — Clouds, rain, grass, cattle, raven chicks. Not the horse. Not the legs. The AWE-people — who wait on loyal-love. — Zion — your gates lock now. Fat of the wheat. His word RUNS. Snow, frost, hail — and one word thaws it. — And the Word came to Jacob. Hallelujah.*

Here's what to take. Two verses. Three and four. *He heals the brokenhearted. He counts the stars.* Tonight — go outside. Look up. Pick one star. He knows its name. Then put your hand on your chest — on the break. He's wrapping it. *Same afternoon.* Neither one is too small or too big. Neither one is a distraction from the other.

The medic is the astronomer. Hallelujah.

---

*Note on translation: v. 1 — *tov. na'im. na'vah* — "good, pleasant, fitting/becoming" (cf. 133:1; 33:1). v. 2 — *boneh Y'rushalayim YHWH* = "building Jerusalem is the Source" — participle, ongoing; *nidchei Yisrael* = "the pushed-out/outcasts of Israel" (Deut 30:4; Isa 11:12; 56:8). **v. 3 — *ha-rofe li-sh'vurei lev* = "the One healing the broken of heart" (cf. 34:18; Isa 61:1; Luke 4:18); *m'chabbesh l'atz'votam* = "binding up their sorrows/wounds."** v. 4 — *moneh mispar la-kochavim* = "counting a number for the stars"; *l'chullam shemot yikra* = "to all of them names He calls" (Gen 15:5; Isa 40:26). v. 5 — *ein mispar* = "no number" — deliberate contrast with v. 4. v. 6 — *m'oded anavim* = "lifting up the humble." v. 9 — *li-v'nei orev asher yikra'u* = "to the sons of the raven who cry" (Job 38:41; Luke 12:24). v. 10 — *shokei ha-ish* = "the thighs of the man" — athletic/military strength. **v. 11 — *rotzeh YHWH et-y're'av* = "the Source delights in those in AWE of Him" — *yirah* correction; KJV "them that fear him" → those in awe; *ratzon* echoes 145:16, 19.** v. 13 — *b'richei sh'arayich* = "the bars of your gates" (Neh 3:3, 6, 13–15; 7:3). v. 14 — *chelev chittim* = "fat of wheat" (cf. 81:16; Deut 32:14). v. 15 — *yarutz d'varo* = "His word runs." v. 16–18 — *sheleg ka-tzamer* = "snow like wool"; *k'for ka-efer* = "frost like ashes"; *karcho k'fittim* = "His ice like morsels/pebbles"; *yishlach d'varo v'yamsem* = "He sends His word and melts them." v. 19–20 — *maggid d'varav l'Ya'akov* = "declaring His words to Jacob" (Deut 4:7–8; Rom 3:1–2; 9:4). ***Yirah* in v. 11 — corrected to awe.***
