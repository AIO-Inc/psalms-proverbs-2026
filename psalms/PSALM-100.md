# PSALM 100 — Enter His Gates with Thanksgiving

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 100:1–5. Five verses. "A psalm for giving thanks" — the only one with that heading. The doxology to the whole enthronement sequence.*

---

Psalms 93–99 built the throne, seated the King, called the nations, got the rivers clapping. Psalm 100 is the *walk-in*. Five verses. Seven commands. Here's how you enter.

**"Shout for joy to the Source — all the earth!"**

Command one. *Shout.* The Hebrew is *hari'u* — the battle cry, the coronation roar. Psalm 47's word. *All the earth* — the whole planet's in the courtyard.

**"Worship the Source with gladness. Come before Him with joyful songs."**

Commands two and three. *Worship — with GLADNESS.* Not duty. Not dread. *Simchah* — joy. The Hebrew for "worship" here is *ivdu* — *serve*. Serve Him *gladly*. The work is a party.

*Come before Him.* Command three. *Bo'u* — enter. Walk in. Psalm 95 — *come, let us*. You're at the gate.

Now — the reason. And it's the center of the psalm:

**"Know that the Source is God. It is He who made us — and we are His. We are His people — the sheep of His pasture."**

*Know.* Command four. *Da'u* — know it. Not feel it. *Know*. Psalm 46 — *be still and know*. This is the *knowing* part.

*He made us — and we are His.* Two facts. *Made.* *His.* You didn't make yourself. You don't own yourself. That's not a burden — it's *relief*. The One who made you is the One who keeps you. Psalm 95 — *our Maker*. Psalm 100 — *and we are His*.

*Sheep of His pasture.* One last time. Psalm 23, 74, 79, 80, 95 — *sheep*. The whole book of Psalms is a flock walking home.

And then — the gate:

**"Enter His gates with thanksgiving — and His courts with praise."**

Command five. *Enter.* Psalm 24 — *lift up your heads, you gates*. Psalm 84 — *I'd rather hold the door*. Psalm 87 — *He loves the gates*. Here you *go through*.

*With thanksgiving.* That's the ticket. Not perfection. Not a clean record. *Todah* — thanks. Psalm 50 — *offer God thanksgiving*. Psalm 95 — *come before Him with thanksgiving*. It's the one thing you're asked to bring. You walk in *saying thank you*.

**"Give thanks to Him. Praise His name."**

Commands six and seven. *Thank. Praise.* Once you're inside — that's the whole job.

And the last verse — *why*:

**"Because the Source is good. His loyal-love endures forever. His faithfulness continues through all generations."**

*Good.* *Tov.* Psalm 92 — *it is good to praise*. Psalm 34 — *taste and see that He is good*. Psalm 73 — *it is good to be near God*. The word that started creation — *and God saw that it was good* — is the reason you walk through the gate.

*Loyal-love forever.* *Chesed l'olam.* The refrain that Psalm 136 will repeat twenty-six times. The thing Psalm 89 thought was lost. *Forever.*

*Faithfulness through all generations.* *Emunah.* Psalm 89's seven. Psalm 78's chain — fathers to children to children yet unborn. The faithfulness *outlasts* you. It was here before you and it'll be here after — and you got to walk through the gate in the middle of it.

Five verses. Seven commands. Here's the shape: *Shout. Serve gladly. Come in. KNOW — He made you, you're His, you're the sheep. Enter the gate with THANKS. Thank Him. Praise His name. Because He's good — loyal-love forever — faithfulness to every generation.*

One hundred psalms. Two-thirds of the way. Book One's two roads. Book Two's deer panting. Book Three's throne in the dirt. Book Four's *the Source reigns*. And now — the gate.

Here's how you walk through it: *thank you*. That's the whole password. Say it and go in.

No* yirah*.*
