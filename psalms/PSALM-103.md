# PSALM 103 — Bless the Source, O My Soul

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 103:1–22. David. The most complete portrait of God's compassion in the Psalms. Not one request in twenty-two verses — only praise. And it has three yirahs, every one of them awe.*

---

Psalm 102 was the man on the roof. Psalm 103 is what you sing when you've come down.

**"Bless the Source, O my soul. All my inmost being — bless His holy name."**

*O my soul.* David's talking to *himself*. Psalm 42's technique — *why are you downcast, my soul?* But now it's not a question. It's an *order*. *Soul — bless Him.* He's commanding the deepest part of himself to get up and praise.

*All my inmost being.* The Hebrew is *kol-k'ravai* — all my insides. Organs. Guts. The part that doesn't have words. *That* part — bless.

**"Bless the Source, O my soul — and forget not all His benefits —"**

*Forget not.* Psalm 78 — *they forgot*. Psalm 103 — *don't*. And then — the list. So you *can't* forget:

**"— who forgives all your sins — and heals all your diseases — who redeems your life from the pit — and crowns you with loyal-love and compassion — who satisfies your desires with good things — so that your youth is renewed like the eagle's."**

Five benefits. *Forgives. Heals. Redeems. Crowns. Satisfies.*

*All your sins.* Not some. *All your diseases.* Same word. *Redeems from the pit* — Psalm 88's pit. Psalm 40's mud. *Crowns you* — Psalm 8's crown on the speck. *Crowns* — not "gives" — *crowns* — with loyal-love and compassion. Two words, one crown.

*Youth renewed like the eagle.* The eagle molts — loses the old feathers, grows new ones — and looks like it did years ago. That's what God does to a worn-out person. New feathers. Isaiah 40 — *they will soar on wings like eagles*.

**"The Source works right-standing and justice for all the oppressed."**

*For the oppressed.* Psalm 72, 82, 94 — always the crushed ones first.

**"He made known His ways to Moses — His deeds to the people of Israel."**

*Made known His ways to Moses.* Exodus 33 — Moses asked *show me Your ways* — and God did. And what He showed him is the next verse:

**"The Source is compassionate and gracious — slow to anger — abounding in loyal-love."**

Exodus 34:6. God's own self-description. Psalm 86 quoted it. Psalm 103 *builds* on it. Four things: *compassionate. gracious. slow to anger. abounding.* This is the sentence God chose when Moses asked *who are You?*

**"He will not always accuse — nor will He harbor His anger forever."**

*Not forever.* Psalm 30 — *His anger is for a moment.* The correction has an *end date*. The love doesn't.

**"He does not treat us as our sins deserve — or repay us according to our wrongdoing."**

*Not as we deserve.* That's the whole gospel in one verse. The math *doesn't balance*. On purpose.

Now — the *distances*. Three of them:

**"As high as the heavens are above the earth — so great is His loyal-love for those who stand in awe of Him."**

*Heavens above earth.* Vertical. Immeasurable. That's the *height* of the love. And who gets it? The ones in *awe* — *yirah*. Not the ones in fear. The ones who *look up* and gasp.

**"As far as the east is from the west — so far has He removed our rebellions from us."**

*East from west.* Horizontal. And here's the thing — north and south have *ends*. You hit the pole. East and west *don't*. You can walk east forever. That's how far the sin's been taken. Not "to the edge." *There's no edge.*

**"As a father has compassion on his children — so the Source has compassion on those who stand in awe of Him."**

*As a father.* Not "as a judge." Not "as a king." *Father.* Psalm 68 — *Father of the fatherless*. The Hebrew is *k'rachem av* — like the *womb-love* of a father. *Rachamim* — compassion — comes from *rechem* — womb. It's the love that's *gut-level*, that you don't choose, that's *in* you because they're yours. God has *that* — for the ones in *awe*. Second *yirah*.

**"Because He knows how we are formed. He remembers that we are dust."**

*He remembers we're dust.* Psalm 78:39 — *He remembered they were flesh.* Here's why the math doesn't balance: He *knows what He's working with*. He shaped the dust. He knows it cracks.

**"The life of mortals is like grass. They flourish like a flower of the field. The wind blows over it and it is gone — and its place remembers it no more."**

*Grass. Flower. Wind. Gone.* Psalm 90, 102 — but here it's not despair. It's *tenderness*. He's saying: *of course You're patient with us. Look how brief we are.*

**"But from everlasting to everlasting — the Source's loyal-love is with those who stand in awe of Him — and His right-standing with their children's children — with those who keep His covenant and remember to obey His precepts."**

Third *yirah*. *Everlasting to everlasting.* Psalm 90 — *from everlasting to everlasting You are God.* The grass is gone in a day. The love is *forever*. And it reaches — *children's children*. Psalm 78's chain.

Now — the throne, and then the *whole choir*:

**"The Source has established His throne in heaven — and His kingdom rules over all."**

Psalm 93. *Reigns.*

**"Bless the Source, you His angels — you mighty ones who do His bidding — who obey His word. Bless the Source, all His heavenly hosts — you His servants who do His will. Bless the Source, all His works — everywhere in His dominion."**

*Angels. Hosts. All His works.* The whole created order — commanded to bless. Psalm 148 will do this in full. Psalm 103 starts it.

And the last line — back to where it started:

**"Bless the Source, O my soul."**

*O my soul.* One more time. He started alone with his own guts. He ended with every angel in heaven. And then — *back to the soul*. Because the whole universe blessing God means nothing if *yours* doesn't.

Twenty-two verses. Not one request. Here's the shape: *Soul — bless Him. Don't forget — He forgives ALL, heals ALL, redeems, crowns, satisfies, renews like an eagle. He's compassionate, gracious, slow to anger, abounding. He won't accuse forever. He doesn't treat us as we deserve. Love high as heaven. Sin removed east-to-west — no edge. Father's womb-love. He remembers we're dust. Grass blows away — love is forever. Angels, hosts, all works — bless Him. Soul — bless Him.*

Command your soul. Then hand it the list.
