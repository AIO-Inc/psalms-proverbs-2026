# PSALM 148 — Praise Him, Sun and Moon

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 148:1–14. Fourteen verses. Third of the closing Hallelujahs. The roll call — every created thing, top to bottom, ordered to praise. Heaven first, then earth. Angels to sea monsters to kings to kids. Francis of Assisi's "Canticle of the Sun" is built on it. So is the Benedicite.*

---

Psalm 147 — *He counts the stars and heals the brokenhearted*. Psalm 148 — *now the stars praise Him back. And so does everything else.* It's a roster. Somebody's calling names, and *nothing* gets to sit out.

Two halves. *From the heavens.* *From the earth.* Watch the order.

**"Hallelujah! Praise the Source from the heavens. Praise Him in the heights above."**

*From the heavens.* *Min ha-shamayim.* The praise starts at the *top*. And it comes *down*. Genesis 1 — creation went *heaven, then earth*. Psalm 148 — the *praise* goes the same direction.

**"Praise Him, all His angels. Praise Him, all His heavenly hosts."**

*Angels.* *Mal'achav.* *Messengers.* *Hosts.* *Tz'va'av.* The *army*. Psalm 103 — *praise the Source, you His angels, you mighty ones who do His bidding*. Psalm 148 — *the whole army — on your feet*.

**"Praise Him, sun and moon. Praise Him, all you shining stars."**

*Sun and moon.* The things every other nation *worshipped*. Egypt — Ra. Babylon — Sin, the moon god. Psalm 148 — *they're not gods. They're CHOIR MEMBERS.* Psalm 136 — *the sun to rule the day, the moon and stars to rule the night*. Psalm 148 — *and they praise the One who set the schedule*.

*Shining stars.* *Kochvei or.* *Stars of light.* Psalm 147 — *He named every one*. Psalm 148 — *and every named one sings*.

**"Praise Him, you highest heavens — and you waters above the skies."**

*Waters above the skies.* Genesis 1:7 — the waters *above the firmament*. The ancient picture of where rain waited. Psalm 148 — *even the water tanks in the sky — praise*.

**"Let them praise the name of the Source — for at His command they were created. He established them for ever and ever — He issued a decree that will never pass away."**

*At His command they were created.* *Ki hu tzivvah v'nivra'u.* *He commanded — and they were.* Psalm 33 — *He spoke, and it came to be*. Psalm 148 — *that's WHY they praise*. Not because they're grateful. Because they *exist*. Existing *is* the praise. The star doesn't have to *decide* to shine. It shines because He said *shine*. That's its Hallelujah.

*A decree that will never pass away.* *Chok-natan v'lo ya'avor.* Some read *a boundary they cannot cross*. Both work. The sun doesn't wander. The moon keeps its schedule. Jeremiah 31 — *the fixed order of moon and stars*. Their *obedience* is their song.

Now — *half two*. Down here:

**"Praise the Source from the earth — you great sea creatures and all ocean depths."**

*Great sea creatures.* *Tanninim.* The *dragons*. The sea monsters. Genesis 1:21 — *the great creatures of the sea*. Psalm 74 — *You crushed the heads of Leviathan*. Psalm 104 — *Leviathan, which You formed to frolic there*. Every other ancient culture — the sea monster is *chaos*, the *enemy of the gods*. Psalm 148 — *the sea monster is in the CHOIR*. First on the earth list. The scariest thing in the ocean — *praise Him*.

Whatever's the *monster* in your life — Psalm 148 says it's a *tenor*. It answers to the same roll call.

*All ocean depths.* *Kol-t'homot.* The *deeps*. Genesis 1:2 — *darkness over the deep*. Psalm 42 — *deep calls to deep*. Psalm 148 — *the deep PRAISES*.

**"Lightning and hail, snow and clouds — stormy winds that do His bidding."**

*Stormy winds that do His bidding.* *Ruach s'arah osah d'varo.* *The storm wind — doing His word.* Psalm 147 — *He sends His word and melts them*. Psalm 148 — *the wind carries out the word*. The hurricane's on the *payroll*. Psalm 29 — *the voice of the Source is over the waters*. Mark 4 — *even the wind and the waves obey Him*. Psalm 148 knew.

**"You mountains and all hills — fruit trees and all cedars."**

*Mountains. Hills.* Psalm 114 — *the mountains skipped like rams*. Psalm 148 — *and they sing*. Isaiah 55 — *the mountains and hills will burst into song, and all the trees will clap their hands*.

*Fruit trees and cedars.* The *useful* tree and the *magnificent* one. The one that feeds you and the one that just stands there being tall. Psalm 92 — *grow like a cedar*. Psalm 1 — *bearing fruit*. Both — *praise*.

**"Wild animals and all cattle — small creatures and flying birds."**

*Wild. Tame. Crawling. Flying.* Genesis 1:24–25 — the same categories. The whole zoo. *Remes* — the *creeping things*. The bugs. The lizards. Psalm 148 — *praise*. Nothing's too small to be on the list. Psalm 147 — *the raven chicks*. Psalm 148 — *and the ants*.

And now — the *last* group. After angels, sun, dragons, storms, cedars, bugs — *us*:

**"Kings of the earth and all nations — you princes and all rulers on earth — young men and women — old men and children."**

*Kings of the earth.* Psalm 2 — the kings *plotting*. Psalm 146 — *don't trust princes*. Psalm 148 — *the princes are in the CHOIR too*. Not leading it. *In* it. Between the cattle and the kids. That's their spot. Psalm 138 — *may all the kings of the earth praise You*. Psalm 148 — *they're called*.

*Young men and women. Old men and children.* *Bachurim v'gam-b'tulot. Z'kenim im-n'arim.* Every age. Every sex. Psalm 8 — *from the lips of children*. Psalm 71 — *even when I am old*. Psalm 148 — *ALL of you*. The teenager and the grandmother. The toddler and the guy in the nursing home. *One choir.*

Notice — humans are *last*. Not first. Not *most important*. *Last* — because we're the ones who *need to be told*. The stars don't need a reminder. The sea monster doesn't skip rehearsal. *We* do. So we get named at the *end* — after everything else has already started singing — so we can *join in*.

**"Let them praise the name of the Source — for His name alone is exalted. His splendor is above the earth and the heavens."**

*His name alone.* *Ki-nisgav sh'mo l'vaddo.* Isaiah 2 — *the Source alone will be exalted in that day*. Psalm 148 — *ALONE*. Not the sun. Not the king. Not the dragon. *His name.*

*Above the earth and the heavens.* Both halves of the psalm. *Above* both. The choir's got two sections — He's over *both*.

And the last verse — the turn from *everything* to *someone*:

**"He has raised up for His people a horn — the praise of all His loyal ones — of Israel, the people close to His heart. Hallelujah!"**

*A horn.* *Keren.* Psalm 132 — *I will make a horn sprout for David*. Psalm 148 — *raised up a horn for His people*. Strength. A king. Luke 1:69 — *a horn of salvation in the house of David*. The choir of everything — ends on the *horn*. The whole universe sings — and then there's *one* note about a rescuer.

*The people close to His heart.* *Am k'rovo.* *The people NEAR Him.* Psalm 145 — *the Source is near to all who call*. Psalm 148 — *and they're near to Him*. It goes both ways. Not *chosen* — *close*. In the front row.

Fourteen verses. Here's the shape: *Hallelujah — from the heavens! Angels. Army. Sun, moon, stars. Waters above. He commanded — you exist — that's your song. — From the earth! Sea monsters. Deeps. Lightning, hail, snow, storm — doing His word. Mountains. Fruit trees, cedars. Wild, tame, crawling, flying. — Kings. Princes. Young, old, kids. — His name ALONE. Above both. — And a horn for the people near His heart. Hallelujah.*

Here's what to do. Go outside. Find one thing from each half. One from the sky — the sun, the moon, a star, a cloud. One from the earth — a tree, a bird, a bug, a rock. And *tell them* verse five — *praise Him — He commanded and you exist*. Then — you're the last name on the list — *do the same thing they're doing*. Out loud. You're late to rehearsal. The dragon's been singing for hours.

Nothing sits out. Hallelujah.

---

*Note on translation: Two-part structure: *min ha-shamayim* (v. 1–6) / *min ha-aretz* (v. 7–14), mirroring Gen 1. v. 2 — *mal'achav* = His messengers/angels; *tz'va'av* = His hosts/army (Ketiv *tz'va'av*, Qere *tz'va'ot*; both preserved). v. 3 — *kochvei or* = "stars of light." v. 4 — *sh'mei ha-shamayim* = "heavens of heavens"; *ha-mayim asher me-al ha-shamayim* = "the waters above the heavens" (Gen 1:7). v. 5 — *ki hu tzivvah v'nivra'u* = "for He commanded and they were created" (cf. 33:9). v. 6 — *chok-natan v'lo ya'avor* = "a statute He gave and it will not pass / they will not transgress" — ambiguous (decree or boundary); both preserved (cf. Jer 31:35–36; 33:20). **v. 7 — *tanninim* = sea monsters/dragons (Gen 1:21; cf. 74:13; 104:26; Isa 27:1; 51:9) — chaos-monsters of ANE myth reassigned as worshippers; *t'homot* = deeps (Gen 1:2).** v. 8 — *ruach s'arah osah d'varo* = "storm-wind doing His word" (cf. 107:25; 147:15, 18). v. 9 — *etz p'ri v'chol-arazim* = "fruit tree and all cedars." v. 10 — *ha-chayyah v'chol-b'hemah remes v'tzippor kanaf* = "the wild beast and all cattle, creeping thing and winged bird" (Gen 1:24–25). v. 11–12 — *malchei-eretz* = kings of earth (cf. 2:2; 138:4); *bachurim v'gam-b'tulot* = "young men and also maidens"; *z'kenim im-n'arim* = "elders with youths." v. 13 — *nisgav sh'mo l'vaddo* = "exalted is His name alone" (Isa 2:11, 17; 12:4). v. 14 — *va-yarem keren l'ammo* = "and He raised a horn for His people" (cf. 75:10; 89:17; 132:17; 1 Sam 2:10; Luke 1:69); *am k'rovo* = "the people near Him" (cf. Deut 4:7; 145:18). Liturgical afterlife: *Benedicite* (Song of the Three, LXX Dan 3:52–90); Francis of Assisi, *Canticle of the Creatures*. No *yirah* in this psalm.*
