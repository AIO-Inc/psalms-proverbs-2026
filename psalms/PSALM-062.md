# PSALM 62 — Only

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 62:1–12. Built on one Hebrew word — ach — "only, alone, surely" — used six times.*

---

There's a word in this psalm that shows up six times, and English usually hides it. *Ach.* It means *only*. *Alone*. *Surely*. *Nothing but.* David uses it like a hammer.

**"Only in God is my soul quiet. From Him comes my rescue."**

*Only.* Not *mostly*. Not *primarily*. *Only.* There's exactly one place the soul gets quiet, and he's naming it.

*Quiet.* The Hebrew is *dumiyah* — stillness, silence, rest. Not the absence of noise — the *presence* of calm. The soul stops running.

**"Only He is my rock and my rescue — my fortress. I will not be greatly shaken."**

*Only.* Second time. Rock. Rescue. Fortress. And then — *not greatly shaken*. Notice he says *greatly*. He might get shaken a little. He's honest. But not *greatly*. Not knocked off.

Now the enemies:

"How long will you attack a man? Will all of you murder him — like a leaning wall, a fence about to fall? **They only plan to topple him from his height.** They delight in lies. With their mouth they bless — but inside, they curse."

*Only.* Third time — but now it's the enemies' *only*. Their whole plan is one thing: *topple him*. That's it. That's all they've got. David's *only* is God. Their *only* is destruction. Two singular focuses. One of them holds.

*A leaning wall.* That's how they see him — already tipping, one push from down. They're wrong about the wall. They don't know what it's leaning *on*.

And then — David repeats verse 1, almost word for word, but he *changes one thing*:

**"Only in God be quiet, my soul — because my hope is from Him."**

Verse 1: *my soul IS quiet.* Verse 5: *BE quiet, my soul.* He went from statement to command. Because between verse 1 and verse 5, the enemies showed up, and the soul that *was* quiet started making noise. So he tells it: *quiet down. Again.*

That's how it works. You don't get quiet once. You get quiet, then the wall-pushers come, then you *tell yourself* to get quiet again. It's a practice, not a possession.

**"Only He is my rock and my rescue — my fortress. I will not be shaken."**

*Only.* Fifth time. And look — verse 2 said *not GREATLY shaken*. Verse 6 says *not shaken*. He dropped the qualifier. Between the first time and the second, he got *more* sure. The repetition tightened the grip.

**"On God rests my rescue and my glory. The rock of my strength — my refuge — is in God."**

Now — the sermon. David turns to the crowd:

**"Trust in Him at all times, you people. Pour out your heart before Him. God is a refuge for us."**

*Pour out your heart.* Like emptying a pitcher. Everything in it — out. On the ground in front of Him. Not the edited version. The whole thing.

*At all times.* Not just crisis. *All.*

**"Only a breath are the children of Adam. A lie — the sons of man. On the scales they go up — together they're lighter than a breath."**

*Only.* Sixth time. Now it's humanity — *only* breath. The Psalm 39 word, *hevel*. Vapor. Put every human on one side of the scale and they *rise* — because they weigh less than the air.

That's not despair. That's *perspective*. The people threatening you — vapor. The people you're afraid of disappointing — vapor. The crowd whose approval you want — *lighter than breath*. Only one weight on the scale that means anything.

**"Don't trust in extortion. Don't set empty hopes on robbery. If wealth increases — don't set your heart on it."**

*If wealth increases.* Not *if you steal it* — *if it comes*. Even honest money. *Don't set your heart on it.* Let it sit in your hand. Not in your chest.

And then the ending — God speaks *twice*, and David hears *two things*:

**"God has spoken once. Twice I have heard this: that strength belongs to God — and to You, Master, belongs loyal-love. Because You repay a man according to his work."**

*Strength — and loyal-love.* Both. Power that could crush you, joined to a love that won't. That's the *only* thing worth leaning the wall on.

Twelve verses. Six *onlys*. *Only in God is my soul quiet. Only He is my rock. They only want to topple me. Only in God — be quiet, soul. Only He is my rock. Only breath — all of them.* Everything else is vapor. One thing isn't.

Find your *only*. Then tell your soul to be quiet — as many times as it takes.
