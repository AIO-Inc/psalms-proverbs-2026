# PSALM 99 — Holy, Holy, Holy

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 99:1–9. The last enthronement psalm. Three times "He is holy" — the structure of the whole thing. And a God who answers.*

---

Psalms 93–98 built the throne. Psalm 99 tells you what the King is *like*. One word. Three times.

**"The Source reigns! Let the nations tremble! He sits enthroned between the cherubim. Let the earth shake!"**

*The Source reigns.* Fourth time in Book Four. *Between the cherubim* — the Ark. The mercy seat. The place in the Holy of Holies where the wings met over the box — *that's* the throne. Not a golden chair in a palace. The lid of the Ark, in the dark, once a year.

*Tremble. Shake.* Not fear — *movement*. The reaction of everything to the presence of Something that big.

**"Great is the Source in Zion. He is exalted over all the nations."**

**"Let them praise Your great and awesome name —**
**He is holy."**

*Awesome.* *Nora.* *Yirah.* Once more — in a verse about *praising*.

*He is holy.* First time. *Kadosh.* Set apart. Other. Not like anything else. The word Isaiah heard the angels shouting — *holy, holy, holy* — Isaiah 6. Psalm 99 has the same *three*.

Now — what holiness *does*:

**"The King is mighty — He loves justice. You have established equity. In Jacob You have done what is just and right."**

*He loves justice.* Not enforces — *loves*. Psalm 33 — *He loves right-standing and justice.* Psalm 37 — *the Source loves justice.* The holiness isn't distant. It's *passionate about fairness*. That's what "set apart" means here — set apart *from* the crooked scale.

**"Exalt the Source our God. Worship at His footstool —**
**He is holy."**

*His footstool.* The Ark again — 1 Chronicles 28:2. The earth as the place His feet rest. You're not at the throne. You're at the *footstool*. Close enough to touch what He stands on.

*He is holy.* Second time.

And now — the psalm does something the other enthronement psalms don't. It gets *personal*. Names:

**"Moses and Aaron were among His priests. Samuel was among those who called on His name. They called on the Source — and He answered them."**

*Moses. Aaron. Samuel.* Three men who *talked to God*. Moses at the bush, on the mountain, face to face. Aaron in the Holy of Holies. Samuel as a boy — *speak, for Your servant is listening*.

*They called — He answered.* That's the pattern. The holy God — the one who makes the earth shake — *answers* when you call. Holiness didn't make Him unreachable. It made Him *reliable*.

**"He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud. They kept His statutes and the decrees He gave them."**

*From the pillar of cloud.* Exodus 13 — the cloud that led them. God *spoke* from it. The same cloud Psalm 97 wrapped around the throne — it *talks*.

**"Source our God — You answered them. You were to Israel a forgiving God — though You punished their misdeeds."**

*A forgiving God — though You punished.* Both. The Hebrew is *El nose* — a God who *carries* — Psalm 25's *nasa*, Psalm 32's *lifted off*. The God who *bears* the sin. And *also* the God who *disciplines*. Psalm 89:32–33 — *I'll punish with the rod — but I won't take My love.* Psalm 94 — *happy is the one You discipline*. Forgiveness *and* correction — that's holiness in practice. Not one or the other.

**"Exalt the Source our God. Worship at His holy mountain —**
**because the Source our God is holy."**

*He is holy.* Third time. The last words.

Three *holys*. Isaiah's seraphim. Revelation 4 — *holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty*. Psalm 99 is the earliest version — the Ark, the footstool, the mountain — and three men who called and got *answered*.

Nine verses. Here's the shape: *The Source reigns — from the mercy seat. His name is awesome — He is holy. He LOVES justice — worship at His footstool — He is holy. Moses, Aaron, Samuel — they called, He ANSWERED. He spoke from the cloud. He forgave — and corrected. He is holy.*

Here's what "holy" means in this psalm: *not too high to answer.* The One the earth shakes before is the One who picked up when Moses called. Set apart — *and* on the line.

Call. He's holy. That's *why* He answers.
