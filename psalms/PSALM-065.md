# PSALM 65 — You Crown the Year

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 65:1–13. A harvest psalm — thanksgiving for rain, grain, and a God who answers.*

---

After eight psalms of enemies, arrows, betrayals, and deserts — this one's a *harvest*. Rain on the fields. Grain in the valleys. The book breathes out.

**"Silence is praise to You, God, in Zion. To You the vow is paid."**

*Silence is praise.* The Hebrew is *dumiyah* — the Psalm 62 word. Sometimes the highest thing you can say to God is *nothing*. Standing in the temple, full, quiet. The silence itself is the offering.

**"You who hear prayer — to You all flesh will come."**

*All flesh.* Everyone. Not the qualified. The ones who need to eat.

**"When wrongdoing overwhelmed us — our rebellions — You covered them."**

*Covered them.* Psalm 32. He does the covering when you stop.

**"Happy is the one You choose and bring near — to live in Your courts. We'll be satisfied with the goodness of Your house — Your holy temple."**

*You choose and bring near.* God does the choosing. God does the bringing. You're not the one who got yourself into the courtyard.

Now — God's power, and it goes from mountains to oceans to *everyone*:

**"By awesome deeds in right-standing You answer us — God of our rescue — the confidence of all the ends of the earth and the farthest seas."**

*The confidence of all the ends of the earth.* Not just Israel's God. The One that people on the far side of the ocean — who've never heard His name — are *leaning on* without knowing it. Every sunrise they count on. Every rain they need. *His.*

"You set the mountains in place by Your strength — girded with power. You still the roaring of the seas — the roaring of their waves — **and the tumult of the peoples.**"

*The tumult of the peoples.* He puts it right next to the roaring seas. Nations in an uproar — same category as waves. He *stills* both. Same verb.

**"The ones who live at the ends of the earth stand in awe of Your signs. You make the going out of the morning and the evening shout for joy."**

*The morning and the evening shout for joy.* Dawn and dusk — *singing*. Twice a day the sky does a thing that makes people at the ends of the earth gasp. He set that up. On purpose. It's His signature, twice daily, for everyone.

Now — the rain. And this is where the psalm goes soft and green:

**"You visit the earth and water it. You enrich it greatly. The river of God is full of water. You provide their grain — because that's how You've prepared it."**

*You visit the earth.* Like a landlord checking on the property — but a good one. Bringing water. Making sure everyone has grain.

**"You drench its furrows. You level its ridges. You soften it with showers. You bless its growth."**

Four verbs about *dirt*. Drench, level, soften, bless. God with His hands in the soil. This isn't the God of Psalm 29 shattering cedars. This is the God who *gardens*.

And then — the line:

**"You crown the year with Your goodness. Your wagon tracks overflow with fatness."**

*You crown the year.* Like a king gets crowned. The year — this year, whatever it's been — gets a *crown* put on it. Not because it earned one. Because that's what He does at the end of the season.

*Your wagon tracks overflow.* Picture God driving a cart across the land — and it's so loaded that it's *spilling*. Fatness pouring out the sides, into the ruts, onto the ground. He's not carefully rationing. He's *dripping*.

**"The pastures of the wilderness overflow. The hills wrap themselves in joy."**

*The wilderness.* Psalm 63's desert — where David thirsted — *overflows*. The dry land gets its turn.

*The hills wrap themselves in joy.* Like putting on a coat. The landscape gets dressed in gladness.

**"The meadows clothe themselves with flocks. The valleys cover themselves with grain. They shout for joy — yes, they sing!"**

*They sing.* The fields. The valleys. The whole earth breaking into a chorus because it's *full*. Psalm 19 said the heavens declare. Psalm 65 says the *ground* sings.

Thirteen verses. Here's the shape: *Silence is praise. You hear. You cover. You bring near. You still the seas and the nations. The morning and evening shout. You visit the earth and get Your hands dirty. You crown the year. Your wagon spills. The desert overflows. The hills put on joy. The valleys sing.*

After the arrows and the caves — go outside. Look at something green. He crowned the year. His wagon's leaking. The hills are wearing joy.

Be quiet. That's praise too.
