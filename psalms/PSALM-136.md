# PSALM 136 — His Loyal-Love Endures Forever

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 136:1–26. Twenty-six verses. The Great Hallel — sung at Passover after the Egyptian Hallel (113–118), and every Sabbath morning in the synagogue to this day. One refrain, twenty-six times. Call and response — a leader speaks the line, the whole room answers. Do not skip the repetitions. The repetition is the psalm.*

---

Here's how this works. One person speaks. Everybody answers. Twenty-six times. By the fifth time you're bored. By the twelfth you're in a rhythm. By the twentieth something's shifted in your chest. By the twenty-sixth you *believe it*.

That's not an accident. That's the *design*. You don't learn *loyal-love endures forever* by hearing it once. You learn it by saying it until it's the only thing left in your mouth.

Say the answer out loud. Every time.

**"Give thanks to the Source — for He is good."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"Give thanks to the God of gods."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"Give thanks to the Master of masters."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

*Loyal-love.* *Chesed.* The covenant word. Not a feeling. A *commitment* that doesn't quit when you do. Psalm 23 — *loyal-love follows me*. Psalm 103 — *as high as the heavens*. Psalm 118 — the *same* refrain, four times. Psalm 136 — *twenty-six*.

Now — *why*. Three reasons. Creation. Exodus. Land. Same three as Psalm 135 — but this time, you *answer* every line.

**"To Him who alone does great wonders."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"Who by His understanding made the heavens."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"Who spread out the earth upon the waters."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"Who made the great lights."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"The sun to rule the day."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"The moon and stars to rule the night."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

Stop there. Sun. Moon. Stars. *Loyal-love.* Why is the sun coming up an act of *loyal-love*? Because He *keeps doing it*. Genesis 8 — *as long as the earth endures, seedtime and harvest, day and night will not cease*. Every sunrise is a covenant kept. Every night the stars come out — that's *chesed*. You woke up today inside a promise.

Now Egypt:

**"To Him who struck down the firstborn of Egypt."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"And brought Israel out from among them."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"With a mighty hand and an outstretched arm."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"To Him who divided the Red Sea asunder."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"And brought Israel through the midst of it."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"But swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

Sit with that one. *Swept Pharaoh into the sea — His loyal-love endures forever.* That's not a comfortable pairing. But it's the *honest* one. Loyal-love to the slaves *meant* the end of the slavemaster. You can't rescue the drowning without dealing with what's holding them under. Psalm 58, 109, 137 — the imprecatory psalms — same logic. His love for you is *fierce* toward what's killing you.

**"To Him who led His people through the wilderness."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

*Through the wilderness.* Forty years of complaining. Psalm 78, 95, 106 — *they tested Him, they forgot Him, they made a calf.* And the answer is still — *His loyal-love endures forever.* Through the wilderness *of their own making*. That's the part that should stop you. The loyal-love didn't endure *because they were good*. It endured *while they weren't*.

Now the land:

**"To Him who struck down great kings."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"And killed mighty kings."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"Sihon king of the Amorites."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"And Og king of Bashan."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"And gave their land as an inheritance."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

**"An inheritance to His servant Israel."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

*Sihon. Og.* Psalm 135 named them too. Two kings who blocked the road. Named *individually* — in a psalm that lists *creation* in three lines. Because the people singing remembered *who was in the way*. Your Sihon has a name. Your Og has a name. Say them. And answer.

Now — the turn from history to *you*:

**"He remembered us in our low estate."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

*Remembered us.* Psalm 105 — *He remembered His covenant*. Psalm 106 — *they forgot*. Psalm 136 — *He remembered US*. Not Abraham. Not Moses. *Us.* The ones singing. Psalm 113 — *He raises the poor from the dust*. He didn't just do it in Egypt. He did it *here*.

*In our low estate.* *B'shiflenu.* When we were *down*. The exile. The barn in Kiriath-Jearim. The bottom. That's where He *remembered*.

**"And freed us from our enemies."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

*Freed.* *Va-yifr'kenu* — He *tore us away*. Psalm 7 — *lest they tear me like a lion*. Here — *He* does the tearing. Rips you *out* of what had you.

And then — the widest line in the psalm:

**"He gives food to every creature."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

*Every creature.* *L'chol-basar* — *to all flesh*. Not Israel. Not the faithful. *All flesh.* Psalm 104 — *they all look to You for food*. Psalm 145 — *You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing*. The dog. The sparrow. The guy who's never once said thank you. *Fed.* That's loyal-love with no membership requirement.

**"Give thanks to the God of heaven."**
*His loyal-love endures forever.*

Twenty-six. The rabbis counted — twenty-six is the numerical value of the Name. *YHWH.* Yod, He, Vav, He — 10 + 5 + 6 + 5. Twenty-six times you say *His loyal-love endures forever* — and you've spelled His name with your breath.

Here's the shape — but there's no shape to give you. The shape is *the refrain*. Everything else is the setup. Creation, Egypt, wilderness, Sihon, Og, the land, our low estate, all flesh. Twenty-six different lines — and *one answer*.

Here's what to do. Take your own list. Twenty-six lines — or six, or ten. *Who made my hands. Who got me out of that house. Who walked me through that year. Who was in the way — by name — and isn't anymore. Who remembered me when I was down. Who fed me when I didn't deserve it.* And after every line — out loud — *His loyal-love endures forever.*

You'll be bored by the fifth. Keep going.

---

*Note on translation: The Great Hallel (*Hallel ha-Gadol*), so named in the Talmud (Pesachim 118a); sung after the Passover meal following 113–118, and at Sabbath morning service. Refrain: *ki l'olam chasdo* = "for to the age [is] His *chesed*" — *chesed* = loyal-love (covenant faithfulness), not "mercy" (KJV) — 26 occurrences. v. 2–3 — *Elohei ha-elohim* / *Adonei ha-adonim* — superlative construct. v. 5 — *bi-t'vunah* = by understanding (Prov 3:19). v. 6 — *l'roka ha-aretz al-ha-mayim* = "who spread the earth over the waters" (cf. 24:2). v. 10 — cf. 135:8. v. 13 — *l'gozer yam-suf li-g'zarim* = "who cut the Reed Sea into cuts/pieces." v. 15 — *v'ni'er* = shook off/swept. v. 17–22 — parallel 135:10–12. v. 23 — *b'shiflenu zachar lanu* = "in our lowness He remembered us" — shift to first-person plural. v. 24 — *va-yifr'kenu* = "and He tore us away" (*parak* — cf. Lam 5:8). v. 25 — *noten lechem l'chol-basar* = "giving bread to all flesh" — universal provision (cf. 104:27; 145:15–16). Gematria: 26 = YHWH (10+5+6+5), noted in rabbinic tradition. No *yirah* in this psalm.*
