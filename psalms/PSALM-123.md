# PSALM 123 — As the Eyes of Servants Look to the Hand

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 123:1–4. Fourth Song of Ascent. Four verses. The shortest of the Ascents. About where you put your eyes when people are laughing at you.*

---

Four verses. One image. *Eyes.*

**"I lift up my eyes to You — to You who sit enthroned in heaven."**

Psalm 121 — *I lift my eyes to the hills.* Psalm 123 — *I lift my eyes to YOU.* Past the hills. Past the city. To the throne. Psalm 93, 99 — *enthroned*. The pilgrim's eyes go *all the way up*.

**"As the eyes of slaves look to the hand of their master — as the eyes of a female slave look to the hand of her mistress — so our eyes look to the Source our God — till He shows us His mercy."**

*The eyes of servants — on the hand.*

Picture a household in the ancient world. The servant doesn't look at the master's *face* — that's presumptuous. Doesn't look at the *floor* — that's absent. Looks at the *hand*. Because the hand *signals*. A flick — come. A gesture — go. A turn of the palm — *now*. The servant who watches the hand is *ready*. Doesn't miss the cue. Doesn't have to be told twice.

That's the posture. Not groveling. *Attentive.* Eyes on the hand — waiting for the *signal*. And the signal they're waiting for is *mercy*. *Till He shows us His mercy.* They're watching for the hand to *move* — and they know it will.

The Hebrew is *ad she-y'chonenu* — *until He is gracious to us*. *Until.* Psalm 94's word. Psalm 105's Joseph — *till the word proved true*. There's an *until*. The hand's going to move. Keep the eyes there.

**"Have mercy on us, Source — have mercy on us — because we have endured no end of contempt."**

*Have mercy.* Twice. *Chonenu. Chonenu.* The servant's request — spoken *to* the hand.

*No end of contempt.* The Hebrew is *rav-savanu vuz* — *much we have been sated with contempt*. *Sated.* Psalm 63 — *satisfied as with rich food.* Psalm 123 — *satisfied with SCORN*. Full up. Can't take another bite.

**"We have endured no end of ridicule from the arrogant — of contempt from the proud."**

*Ridicule from the arrogant.* The Hebrew is *ha-la'ag ha-sha'ananim* — the mockery of the *comfortable*. The ones at ease. The ones who've never had to lift their eyes to anyone's hand — because they *are* the hand. And they *laugh* at the servant watching.

*Contempt from the proud.* Psalm 73's wicked — *pride is their necklace*. Psalm 120's *those who hate peace*. They look at the pilgrim with his eyes up and think — *pathetic. Waiting on a God who won't move.*

And the pilgrim — doesn't look at them. Doesn't answer. *Eyes stay on the hand.*

Four verses. Here's the shape: *Eyes up — past everything — to the throne. Like a servant watching the master's hand — ready — until mercy. Have mercy. Have mercy. We're FULL of their contempt. The comfortable ones laugh. — Eyes on the hand.*

Here's what to do with the shortest Ascent: when they're laughing — don't look at them. Look at the hand. Not the face — you're not demanding. Not the floor — you're not giving up. The *hand*. Ready for the signal. And you know it's coming — because there's an *until*.

The proud have never had to watch a hand. That's their loss. The one who watches — *sees it move*.
