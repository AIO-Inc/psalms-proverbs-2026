# PSALM 139 — Awesomely and Wonderfully Made

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 139:1–24. Twenty-four verses. Attributed to David. The most intimate psalm in the book — God knows you completely, is everywhere you go, and made you by hand in the dark. Contains the most-quoted mistranslation about human worth in the English Bible: "fearfully made." Corrected here.*

---

Psalm 138 ended — *don't abandon the work of Your hands.* Psalm 139 is the work of His hands *talking back*. And realizing it was never alone for a second.

**"Source — You have searched me and You know me. You know when I sit and when I rise. You perceive my thoughts from afar."**

*Searched me.* *Chakartani.* Dug into. Investigated. Not glanced at — *excavated*. Psalm 7 — *God searches minds and hearts*. Psalm 17 — *You probe my heart*. And the result — *You KNOW me*. *Yada.* The deepest word for knowing in Hebrew. Genesis 4 — *Adam knew Eve*. Not information. *Intimacy.*

*Thoughts from afar.* Psalm 138 — *the proud He knows from afar*. Psalm 139 — *my thoughts from afar*. Same distance. Different knowing. He knows the proud man's *position*. He knows *your* *thoughts*.

**"You discern my going out and my lying down — You are familiar with all my ways. Before a word is on my tongue — You know it completely, Source."**

*Before a word is on my tongue.* He hears the sentence before you finish forming it. Which means He heard the thing you *almost* said and didn't. He heard the prayer you couldn't get out. Romans 8 — *the Spirit intercedes with groans too deep for words*. Psalm 139 — *You know it completely — before it's a word.*

**"You hem me in — behind and before — You have laid Your hand upon me. Such knowledge is too wonderful for me — too lofty for me to attain."**

*Hem me in.* *Tzartani.* The root is *tzar* — *narrow*, *pressed*, *besieged*. It's the *trouble* word — Psalm 138's *midst of trouble*. David uses it *for God*. God's got him *surrounded*. *Behind and before.* Nowhere to go.

And then — *You've laid Your hand on me.* Not a fist. A *hand*. The siege is a *hold*.

*Too wonderful for me.* Psalm 131 — *things too wonderful for me* — the things he stopped pacing in. Here — *being known this completely* is one of those things. He can't grasp it. So he stops trying and just — *sits in it*.

Now — the *everywhere*:

**"Where can I go from Your Spirit? Where can I flee from Your presence? If I go up to the heavens — You are there. If I make my bed in the grave — You are there. If I rise on the wings of the dawn — if I settle on the far side of the sea — even there Your hand will guide me — Your right hand will hold me fast."**

*Where can I flee.* Notice — he's *asking*. Part of him wants to run. That's honest. Being fully known is *terrifying* before it's comforting. Jonah tried the far side of the sea. Psalm 139 says *He was on the boat.*

*If I make my bed in Sheol — You are there.* The *grave*. The place of silence. Psalm 6 — *who praises You from the grave?* Psalm 88 — *the dead You remember no more*. Psalm 139 — *You're THERE*. Down where the psalms kept saying God *wasn't*. He is. Psalm 16 — *You won't abandon me to the grave*. Psalm 139 — *and if I went — You'd already be down there*.

*The wings of the dawn.* The first light shooting across the sky — *ride it*. East as far as east goes. *The far side of the sea* — west as far as west goes. Both ends of the map. *Even there — Your hand.* Psalm 138 — *Your right hand saves me*. Psalm 139 — *Your right hand holds me fast — at the edge of the world*.

**"If I say: 'Surely the darkness will hide me — and the light become night around me' — even the darkness will not be dark to You. The night will shine like the day — for darkness is as light to You."**

*The darkness will hide me.* The last hiding place. Not a *location* — a *condition*. The thing you do in the dark. The thought you have with the lights off. Psalm 90 — *our secret sins in the light of Your presence*. Psalm 139 — *darkness is as light to You*. There's no dimmer switch He can't see through.

Sit with that a second. It sounds like a threat. It isn't. Because — verse thirteen.

**"For You created my inmost being. You knit me together in my mother's womb."**

*You knit me.* *T'sukkeni.* *Wove* me. Like fabric on a loom. Psalm 138 — *the work of Your hands*. Psalm 139 — *here's how You did it. Thread by thread.* In a place *nobody* could see — the womb — the dark — and *He was there*. That's why the darkness is light to Him. *He works in it.* He *started* you in it.

Now — the verse. The one on every baby announcement, every Christian coffee mug — and the one *nobody's read right*:

**"I praise You — because I am awesomely and wonderfully made. Your works are wonderful — I know that full well."**

*Awesomely made.* The Hebrew is *nora'ot* — from *yirah*. The King James wrote *fearfully*. Every English Bible since — *fearfully and wonderfully made*. And generations of people have grown up thinking their bodies were made with *fear* in them. That God made them *trembling*. Or that they should be *afraid* of what they are.

No. *Nora* is the awe-word. Psalm 47 — *the Source Most High is awesome*. Psalm 65 — *awesome deeds*. Psalm 66 — *how awesome are Your works*. Psalm 111 — *the awe of the Source is the beginning of wisdom*. It's what you feel at the Grand Canyon. At a newborn. At the ocean at night. *Awe.* Not fear.

*I am AWESOMELY made.* You were made in a way that should stop people's breath. The One who made the heavens and the wings of the dawn — made *you* — with the same hand — and it's *awe-inspiring* work. That's what the verse says. Not *fearfully*. *Awesomely.* Correct it every time you hear it wrong.

*I know that full well.* *Nafshi yoda'at m'od.* *My soul knows — very much.* Not *I think*. *I KNOW.* Psalm 135 — *I know the Source is great*. Psalm 139 — *I know I'm awesomely made*. Same knowing.

**"My frame was not hidden from You — when I was made in the secret place. When I was woven together in the depths of the earth — Your eyes saw my unformed body. All the days ordained for me were written in Your book — before one of them came to be."**

*Woven in the depths of the earth.* The womb as a *mine*. Dark, deep, underground — and He's *embroidering* down there. *Rukkamti* — the word for the *embroidered* curtains of the Tabernacle. Exodus 26. You were made with the same craft as the *holy place*.

*Your eyes saw my unformed body.* *Golmi.* My *unformed mass*. Before shape. Before face. A *lump*. And His eyes were *on* it. He saw the embryo and didn't look away. He saw *you* before you were *anything* — and *kept knitting*.

*All the days written.* Psalm 56 — *my tears in Your record*. Psalm 139 — *my days in Your book*. Written *before one came to be*. You have never lived a day He hadn't already turned the page to.

**"How precious to me are Your thoughts, God! How vast is the sum of them! Were I to count them — they would outnumber the grains of sand. When I awake — I am still with You."**

*Your thoughts about me.* Psalm 40 — *Your thoughts toward us cannot be counted*. Psalm 139 — *more than sand*. Every grain on every beach — *not enough*. He's been thinking about you longer than you've been alive to think about anything.

*When I awake — still with You.* Psalm 3 — *I lie down, I sleep, I wake — the Source sustains me*. Psalm 17 — *when I awake, I'll see Your face*. Psalm 139 — *You were there in the dark. I open my eyes — You're still here.*

Now — the turn. And people skip it, because it's ugly after all that beauty:

**"If only You, God, would slay the mark-missers! Away from me — you who are bloodthirsty! They speak of You with evil intent — Your adversaries misuse Your name. Do I not hate those who hate You, Source? Do I not loathe those who rise up against You? I have nothing but hatred for them — I count them my enemies."**

Why is this *here*? After the womb? After the sand?

Because David's been laid bare — *You know me completely* — and now he's *showing what's in there*. Including the hate. He doesn't hide it. He can't — verse twelve — *darkness is as light to You*. So he *says* it. Psalm 137's container again. *I hate the ones who hate You.* It's in me. Here it is. *You already knew.*

That's not the psalm endorsing hatred. It's the psalm refusing to *fake* its absence. And watch what comes next:

**"Search me, God — and know my heart. Test me — and know my anxious thoughts. See if there is any offensive way in me — and lead me in the way everlasting."**

*Search me.* Verse one — *You HAVE searched me*. Verse twenty-three — *SEARCH me*. He knew he was known. Now he *asks* for it. That's the whole psalm in one turn. From *You know me — and I can't escape it* to *know me — and I don't WANT to escape it*.

*See if there is any offensive way in me.* Right after *I hate them*. He's *handing over the hate*. *Look at it. If it's wrong — fix it.* That's what you do with the ugly verse. You don't cut it. You hand it up and say *search THIS*.

*Lead me in the way everlasting.* *Derech olam.* Psalm 1 — *the way*. Psalm 119 — *a lamp to my foot*. Psalm 139 — *the way that lasts*. He wants to be *led*. Not right. *Led.*

Twenty-four verses. Here's the shape: *You searched me — You KNOW me. Before the word's on my tongue. I'm hemmed in — Your hand's on me. Where could I go? Heaven — You're there. The grave — You're there. Wings of the dawn — the far side of the sea — the dark — all light to You. Because YOU knit me. In the womb. In the mine. I am AWESOMELY made — and I know it. You saw the lump and kept weaving. My days in Your book. Your thoughts — more than sand. I wake — You're still here. — And yes, there's hate in me. Here it is. SEARCH me. Lead me in the way that lasts.*

Here's what to do. Say verse fourteen out loud — *correctly*. *I am awesomely and wonderfully made.* Not fearfully. *Awesomely.* Then — if you've got a verse nineteen in you — say that too. To Him. Then — verse twenty-three. *Search me.* All of it. He already saw it in the dark. Now you're *letting* Him.

You were embroidered in a mine by a God who thinks about you more than there's sand. Act like it.

---

*Note on translation: v. 1 — *chakartani* = You have searched/investigated me; *va-teda* = and You know (*yada* — intimate knowledge). v. 2 — *me-rachok* = from afar (cf. 138:6). v. 5 — *tzartani* = You have hemmed/besieged me — root *tzur*, cf. *tzarah* (distress) — deliberately reclaims siege language for divine protection. v. 6 — *pil'iyah* = wonderful/beyond (cf. 131:1 *nifla'ot*). v. 8 — *Sheol* = the grave, never hell. v. 9 — *kanfei-shachar* = wings of dawn; *acharit yam* = far side of the sea (west). v. 12 — *ka-chashechah ka-orah* = "as the darkness, so the light." v. 13 — *t'sukkeni* = You wove/knit me (*sakak* — cover/weave). **v. 14 — *nora'ot nifleiti* = "awesomely [and] wonderfully I was made" — *nora'ot* is the niphal participle of *yare*, the *yirah* root; KJV "fearfully" is the project's central correction applied to the human body: render "awesomely." Same root as 47:2; 65:5; 66:3, 5; 68:35; 76:7; 96:4; 99:3; 111:9.** *Nafshi yoda'at m'od* = "my soul knows very much." v. 15 — *rukkamti* = I was embroidered (*rakam* — Exod 26:36; 27:16; 36:37 — Tabernacle curtains); *b'tachtiyyot aretz* = "in the depths of the earth" — womb as mine. v. 16 — *golmi* = my unformed mass/embryo (only occurrence in HB; Mishnaic *golem*). v. 18 — *hekitzoti* = I awake — some emend to "I come to an end," both preserved. v. 19–22 — imprecatory section; *r'sha'im* = mark-missers/wicked; *ansh'ei damim* = men of blood(s). v. 21 — *m'san'echa* = those who hate You. v. 23 — *chokreni* = search me — repeats v. 1's root as imperative; *sar'appai* = my anxious/disquieting thoughts (cf. 94:19). v. 24 — *derech-otzev* = way of pain/idolatry/offense (ambiguous — both preserved); *derech olam* = the everlasting way. ***Yirah* in v. 14 (*nora'ot*) — corrected to awe.***
