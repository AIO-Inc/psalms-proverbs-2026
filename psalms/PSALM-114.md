# PSALM 114 — The Mountains Skipped Like Rams

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 114:1–8. Eight verses. The Exodus told as the earth's reaction — the sea ran, the river backed up, the mountains jumped. And a question asked directly to the water.*

---

Second Hallel psalm. Sung before the Passover meal. The whole Exodus in eight verses — and the *landscape* is the main character.

**"When Israel came out of Egypt — the house of Jacob from a people of foreign tongue —"**

*A people of foreign tongue.* They couldn't understand their masters. Four hundred years in a place where the language wasn't theirs. That's exile — not just captivity. *Not being understood.*

**"— Judah became God's sanctuary. Israel His dominion."**

*Became His sanctuary.* Not "built a sanctuary." *Became* one. The *people* were the holy place — before there was a temple. Psalm 90 — *You have been our dwelling place.* Here — *we're His.* 1 Corinthians 3 — *you are the temple.*

Now — the earth reacts. And it's *physical comedy*:

**"The sea looked and fled. The Jordan turned back."**

*The sea LOOKED.* Saw something. Saw *Who* was coming. And *ran*. Psalm 77 — *the waters saw You and writhed*. Here — *fled*. The Red Sea didn't part. It *bolted*.

*The Jordan turned back.* Forty years later — Joshua 3 — the river stopped and *reversed*. Two bodies of water. Two retreats. Same cause.

**"The mountains leaped like rams. The hills like lambs."**

*Leaped like rams.* Sinai — Exodus 19 — *the whole mountain trembled violently*. Psalm 29 — *He makes Lebanon skip like a calf*. But here it's *rams and lambs*. A *flock* of mountains. *Bouncing.* Not in terror — in the way young animals bounce when the shepherd shows up. The hills are *excited*.

Psalm 68 — *why do you look with envy, you many-peaked mountains?* Psalm 114 — they've stopped being jealous. They're *playing*.

And now — the psalmist stops narrating and *talks to the geography*:

**"Why was it, sea, that you fled? Why, Jordan, did you turn back? Why, mountains, did you leap like rams? You hills — like lambs?"**

*Why, sea?* He's *interviewing the water*. *What did you see? What made you run?* Four questions to four features. Like a reporter at the scene.

And they don't answer. Because the answer's obvious. Verse 7:

**"Tremble, earth, at the presence of the Master — at the presence of the God of Jacob —"**

*At the presence.* That's why. Not a command. Not a threat. *Presence.* He *showed up* — and the sea *left*. The river *reversed*. The mountains *bounced*. Nothing was ordered. Everything *reacted*.

That's what presence does. You don't have to be told to move when the King walks in. Your legs do it.

**"— who turned the rock into a pool — the hard rock into springs of water."**

The last verse. After the sea running and the mountains leaping — *water from rock*. Exodus 17. Numbers 20. Psalm 78, 105. The thing that *shouldn't* give water — *gave it*. The hardest surface in the desert — a *spring*.

Look at the pattern. The sea — *should* be water — *fled*. The rock — *shouldn't* be water — *poured*. God reverses both. Where you expect the flow, He dries it. Where you expect the stone, He opens it.

If you're the rock — hard, dry, the one nobody expects anything from — this is the verse. *The hard rock into springs.*

Eight verses. Here's the shape: *Out of Egypt — from people who couldn't understand them. They BECAME the sanctuary. The sea saw and RAN. The Jordan reversed. The mountains bounced like rams. — Why, sea? Why, mountains? — Presence. He walked in. And the rock became a spring.*

Jesus sang this before the last meal. *The sea fled at His presence.* Then He went to the garden. Then the soldiers came. And for one moment — John 18:6 — *they drew back and fell to the ground* when He said *I am He.*

The sea's not the only thing that steps back when He shows up.
