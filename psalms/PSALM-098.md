# PSALM 98 — Let the Rivers Clap Their Hands

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 98:1–9. The psalm behind "Joy to the World." Isaac Watts wrote the carol from these nine verses — not from the Christmas story.*

---

Every December you sing this and don't know it. *Joy to the World* isn't from Luke. It's from Psalm 98.

**"Sing to the Source a new song — because He has done marvelous things. His right hand and His holy arm have worked salvation for Him."**

*A new song.* Third time in Book Four. Because *He did something* — the Hebrew is *nifla'ot*, wonders — and the old song won't cover it.

*His right hand and His holy arm.* Not an army. Not a general. *His own arm.* Isaiah 52 — *the Source has bared His holy arm in the sight of all the nations.* He rolled up His sleeve and did it Himself.

**"The Source has made His salvation known — and revealed His right-standing to the nations."**

*Made known. Revealed.* Past tense. Not "will someday." *Has.* Psalm 97 — *all peoples see His glory*. Here it's *done*. The nations know.

**"He has remembered His loyal-love and His faithfulness to Israel."**

*Remembered.* Psalm 89 asked *where is Your loyal-love?* Psalm 98 answers: *He remembered it.* Psalm 78 — the people forgot, *He remembered*. The word that saves everything.

**"All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God."**

*All the ends.* The far edges. Psalm 97's distant islands. *Have seen.* Not heard about — *seen*. Luke 2 — *all flesh shall see the salvation of God.* Simeon holding the baby — *my eyes have seen Your salvation.* He was quoting *this*.

Now — the noise. And it escalates:

**"Shout for joy to the Source, all the earth! Burst into jubilant song with music!"**

*Burst.* The Hebrew is *pitzchu* — break out, break forth. Like a dam. Like something that's been held in and *can't* be anymore.

**"Make music to the Source with the harp — with the harp and the sound of singing — with trumpets and the blast of the ram's horn — shout for joy before the Source, the King!"**

Harp. Voices. Trumpets. Ram's horn. Every instrument they had. *The King.* Psalm 93, 96, 97 — enthronement. The coronation band's playing.

And then — creation joins. Psalm 96 gave the trees a part. Psalm 98 gives it to the *water*:

**"Let the sea resound — and everything in it! The world — and all who live in it!"**

**"Let the rivers clap their hands! Let the mountains sing together for joy!"**

*The rivers clap their hands.*

Picture it. Every river on earth — the Nile, the Jordan, the Euphrates, the one behind your house — *applauding*. The Hebrew is *yimcha'u-kaf* — strike the palm. The water hitting the banks, the rapids over rocks — that sound *is* the clapping. It always was. You just didn't know what it was for.

*The mountains sing together.* Psalm 97 melted them like wax. Psalm 98 has them in a *choir*. Same mountains. When the King comes — what was terrifying becomes *harmony*.

**"— before the Source — because He comes to judge the earth."**

*He comes to judge.* Psalm 96's ending. And the rivers are *clapping* about it. Because *judgment* — in this psalm — isn't the bad news. It's the *good* news. The crooked courts get fixed. The widow gets heard. Psalm 82's failed gods get replaced by the real Judge — and *creation applauds*.

**"He will judge the world in right-standing — and the peoples with equity."**

*Equity.* Straight. Fair. The last word.

Nine verses. Here's the shape: *New song — He did it with His own arm. He REMEMBERED. All the ends of the earth have SEEN it. Burst — harp, trumpet, horn — for the King. Sea, roar. Rivers, CLAP. Mountains, sing. He's coming to set it straight.*

*Joy to the world — the Lord is come. Let earth receive her King.* Watts had it right. *Let every heart prepare Him room — and heaven and nature sing.*

Heaven and *nature*. The rivers already know. Clap with them.

*Note on translation: v. 1 —* nifla'ot *= wonders, marvelous things;* z'ro'a kodsho *= His holy arm (Isa 52:10, 53:1). v. 2 —* hodia YHWH y'shuato *= "YHWH has made known His salvation." v. 3 —* zachar *= He remembered — answer to Ps 89:49. v. 3 —* ra'u kol-afsei-aretz *= "all ends of the earth have seen" — cf. Luke 2:30–31, 3:6. v. 4 —* pitzchu *= break forth. v. 8 —* n'harot yimcha'u-kaf *= "rivers clap palm";* harim yachad y'ran'nu *= mountains together sing for joy. v. 9 —* b'meisharim *= with equity/uprightness. Basis of Isaac Watts' "Joy to the World" (1719). No* yirah*.*
