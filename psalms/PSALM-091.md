# PSALM 91 — Under His Wings

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 91:1–16. No author named — tradition says Moses, continuing from 90. The shelter psalm. Soldiers carry it. Satan quoted it to Jesus. God speaks the last three verses Himself.*

---

Psalm 90 said you're morning grass. Psalm 91 says where the grass can hide.

**"Whoever dwells in the shelter of the Most High — will rest in the shadow of the Almighty."**

*Dwells.* Lives there. Not visits. *Rests* — the Hebrew is *yitlonan* — spends the night. Overnight in the shadow. You don't run to a shelter in the morning. You *sleep* there.

**"I will say of the Source: 'He is my refuge and my fortress — my God, in whom I trust.'"**

*I will say.* Out loud. The psalm's telling you to *declare* it — because the declaration is part of the dwelling.

**"Surely He will save you from the fowler's snare — and from the deadly pestilence."**

*The fowler.* The bird-trapper. Hidden nets, baited hooks. The dangers you don't see until you're in them. *And pestilence* — the dangers nobody can fight. Both covered.

**"He will cover you with His feathers — and under His wings you will find refuge. His faithfulness will be your shield and rampart."**

*Feathers.* Psalm 17, 36, 57, 61, 63 — the wings, again and again. But here it's *feathers* — the soft part. Not the armor of the wing. The *down*. The warmth against the skin. God as the mother bird — and you as the chick that can't see anything but feathers and dark and *safe*.

**"You will not fear the terror of night — nor the arrow that flies by day — nor the pestilence that stalks in the darkness — nor the plague that destroys at midday."**

*Night. Day. Darkness. Midday.* All four quarters of the clock. There's no hour that's not covered.

**"A thousand may fall at your side — ten thousand at your right hand — but it will not come near you."**

*A thousand. Ten thousand.* He's not saying the danger isn't real. People *are* falling. He's saying — *not you. Not here. Not under the feathers.*

**"You will only observe with your eyes — and see the punishment of the wicked."**

*Observe.* A spectator. Watching from the shelter.

**"If you say: 'The Source is my refuge' — and you make the Most High your dwelling — no harm will overtake you. No disaster will come near your tent."**

*If you say.* The condition — again — is *saying it*. Making Him the *dwelling*. Not the emergency exit. The *home address*.

Now — the angels:

**"Because He will command His angels concerning you — to guard you in all your ways. They will lift you up in their hands — so that you will not strike your foot against a stone."**

*Command His angels.* Orders. Given. About *you*. Psalm 34 — *the angel camps around*. Here they're *assigned*.

*So you won't strike your foot.* This is the verse Satan quoted to Jesus in the wilderness. *Throw Yourself off the temple — it's written, the angels will catch You.* And Jesus said: *It's also written — don't put God to the test.* The promise is real. It's not a *stunt*. You don't jump to prove the feathers exist. You *dwell*. There's a difference — and Jesus drew it.

**"You will tread on the lion and the cobra. You will trample the great lion and the serpent."**

*Tread on.* Not avoid. *Step on.* The thing that hunts you becomes the thing under your foot. Luke 10 — *I've given you authority to trample snakes and scorpions.* Same image.

And now — God takes the microphone. First person. Last three verses:

**"'Because he loves Me — I will rescue him. I will protect him — because he acknowledges My name.'"**

*Because he loves Me.* Not "because he earned it." Not "because he's righteous." *Loves.* The Hebrew is *chashak* — to be attached, to cling with desire. The one who's *stuck on* God — God rescues.

*Acknowledges My name.* Knows it. Says it. Psalm 91:2 — *I will say*. The saying was the acknowledging.

**"'He will call on Me — and I will answer him. I will be with him in trouble. I will deliver him and honor him.'"**

*With him IN trouble.* Not "I'll keep him out of trouble." *IN* it. The shelter isn't a place trouble can't find. It's the place where God's *standing next to you* when it does.

**"'With long life I will satisfy him — and show him My salvation.'"**

*Satisfy.* Psalm 63, 90 — the fat-and-marrow word. *Long life* — and Psalm 90 just said seventy, eighty. So "long" isn't a number. It's *full*. A life you're *satisfied* with, whatever the count.

*Show him My salvation.* The last word. Not "give him." *Show.* Let him *see it*. Psalm 27 — *I'll see the goodness of the Source in the land of the living.*

Sixteen verses. Here's the shape: *Sleep in the shadow. SAY He's your refuge. Feathers over you. Night, day, darkness, noon — all covered. Thousands fall — not you. Angels assigned. Step on the snake. And God says: because he loves Me — I'm with him IN trouble — I'll show him salvation.*

Soldiers have carried this psalm into every war since it was written. Plague victims prayed it. It's been folded in pockets and tattooed on arms.

Here's what it's actually for: *dwelling*. Not a magic shield. A *home address*. Under the feathers. Say it out loud. Sleep there. And when the trouble comes — He's *in* it with you.
