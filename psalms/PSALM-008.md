# PSALM 8 — What Is Man?

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 8:1–9.*

---

Go outside tonight. Get away from the streetlights if you can. Look up.

That's where this psalm was written.

"Source, our Master — how majestic is Your name across the whole earth. You've set Your glory above the heavens."

Above the heavens. Above the stars. He's not *in* the sky — the sky is the floor He stands on.

"From the mouths of babies and nursing infants You've established strength — to silence the ones who fight You."

Think about that. The most powerful argument for God isn't a philosopher. It's a baby. A newborn making noise. The sheer *existence* of a new human being shuts the critics up faster than any debate. Life itself is the evidence.

Now the look-up moment:

"When I look at Your heavens, the work of Your fingers — the moon and the stars You put in place —"

Fingers. Not fists, not arms. *Fingers.* The universe is fine work. Detail work. Something a craftsman does bent over a bench.

"— what is man, that You would think about him? What is the son of Adam, that You would pay attention to him?"

This is the question. Standing under the whole night sky, feeling about the size of a speck. *Why would You bother with me?*

And here's the answer — and it's not what you'd expect. You'd expect: "You're nothing." Instead:

**"You made him a little lower than the heavenly ones. You crowned him with glory and honor."**

Crowned. Glory. Honor. The speck under the stars is wearing a *crown*.

"You made him ruler over the work of Your hands. You put everything under his feet — all the flocks and herds, the wild animals, the birds of the air, the fish of the sea, everything that moves along the ocean paths."

You're not a speck. You're a steward. The One who set the stars in place with His fingers turned around and handed *you* the keys to the planet. Not because you're big. Because He decided you'd be trusted.

That's the whole psalm: You are impossibly small, and impossibly honored, at the same time. Both are true. Hold both.

The smallness keeps you humble. The crown keeps you upright.

"Source, our Master — how majestic is Your name across the whole earth."

He starts and ends with the same line. Because after you've seen the stars and remembered the crown, there's nothing left to say except *wow*.

Go outside. Look up. Remember who made it. Then remember who He handed it to.

---

*Note on translation: v. 5 —* elohim *here can mean "God" or "heavenly beings/angels"; the Septuagint and Hebrews 2:7 read "angels." Rendered "heavenly ones." "Son of man" =* ben adam *= son of Adam, a human being. "Dominion" =* mashal *= stewardship/governance, not exploitation. No* yirah*. No corrections needed — this psalm was never weaponized.*
