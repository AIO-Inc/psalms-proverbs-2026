# PSALM 93 — The Source Reigns

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 93:1–5. Five verses. The first of the enthronement psalms (93, 95–99) — the answer to Psalm 89's empty throne. The throne was never empty. It just wasn't David's.*

---

Book Three ended with the king's crown in the dirt. Book Four's answer, in three words:

**"The Source reigns."**

*Malak YHWH.* He is King. Present tense. Not "will reign." Not "used to." *Reigns.* Right now. While the earthly throne sits empty.

That's the whole point of Psalms 93 through 99. Israel lost its king — and discovered it had never *needed* one. The real throne was never in Jerusalem. It was *above* it.

**"He is robed in majesty. The Source is robed. He is armed with strength."**

*Robed.* Dressed. The Hebrew is *lavesh* — to put on clothing. God *wearing* majesty like a garment. Like a king getting dressed for the throne room. The clothes *are* Him — but the image is *deliberate*. He's *ready*.

**"Indeed — the world is established, firm and secure."**

*Established.* Psalm 96, Psalm 24 — the earth set on the waters. Because the King is on His throne — the *ground holds*. Psalm 82 said when the powerful fail, *the foundations shake*. Psalm 93 says when the *real* King reigns — *they don't*.

**"Your throne was established long ago. You are from all eternity."**

*Long ago.* Before David's throne. Before Psalm 89's promise. *From eternity.* Psalm 90 — *before the mountains were born*. The throne Ethan mourned was a *copy*. The original's been occupied since before there was a *before*.

Now — the sea:

**"The seas have lifted up, Source — the seas have lifted up their voice — the seas have lifted up their pounding waves."**

*Three times.* *The seas have lifted up.* Rising, rising, rising. The Hebrew is *n'harot* — rivers, floods — the chaos waters. Psalm 74's Leviathan. Psalm 89's Rahab. The thing that threatens to swallow everything — *getting louder*.

That's the sound of 586 BC. Babylon's army. The world coming apart. The waves *roaring*.

**"Mightier than the thunder of the great waters — mightier than the breakers of the sea — the Source on high is mighty."**

*Mightier.* The waves lift their voice. The Source is *louder*. Not by a little. Psalm 29 — *the voice of the Source is over the waters*. The chaos makes noise. The King makes *more*.

You don't have to silence the waves. You have to hear what's *above* them.

**"Your statutes, Source, stand firm. Holiness adorns Your house for endless days."**

*Your statutes stand firm.* The waves rise and fall. The *word* doesn't move. Psalm 12 — *silver refined seven times*. Psalm 19 — *the instruction of the Source is complete*. When everything's water — the *word* is the rock.

*Holiness adorns Your house.* The temple burned. But *His* house — the real one — is still *decorated*. Still holy. Still standing. *For endless days.*

Five verses. Here's the shape: *The Source REIGNS. He's dressed for it. The world holds because He's on the throne. That throne's older than the mountains. The seas are rising — three times — roaring. He's LOUDER. His word stands. His house is holy forever.*

When the earthly throne goes empty — when the leader fails, the institution collapses, the thing you trusted turns out to be grass — say the three words. *The Source reigns.* He's been dressed for it since before the floods started.
