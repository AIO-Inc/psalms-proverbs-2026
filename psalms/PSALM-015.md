# PSALM 15 — Who Gets to Stay?

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 15:1–5. Five verses. A checklist for the kind of person who can stand in God's presence.*

---

One question. Then the answer. That's the whole psalm.

"Source, who gets to be a guest in Your tent? Who gets to stay on Your holy mountain?"

Guest. The Hebrew is *gur* — to sojourn, to be taken in as a stranger and treated like family. Who gets *that*? Who gets to move in?

And notice the answer isn't about religion. Not one word about sacrifices, festivals, rituals, or doctrine. Every single line is about how you treat *people*.

**"The one who walks with integrity."** Whole. Not one person at church and another at work. The same all the way through.

**"And does what's right."** Not talks about it. *Does* it.

**"And speaks truth in his heart."** Not just to others — to *himself*. The one who doesn't lie to himself about who he is or what he did. That's the hardest honesty there is.

**"Whose tongue doesn't slander."** Doesn't tear people down when they're not in the room.

**"Who does no harm to his neighbor."** The person next to you — literally. The one whose life touches yours.

**"And doesn't pick up a reproach against his friend."** Doesn't *pick it up* — doesn't carry the gossip, doesn't repeat the story, doesn't help the reputation-killer do his work.

**"In whose eyes the corrupt person is despised — but he honors the ones who stand in awe of the Source."** Gets his admiration pointed the right way. Not impressed by the guy who got rich doing damage. Impressed by the quiet one who never lost the wonder.

**"Who swears to his own hurt and doesn't change."** Made a promise. Turned out to be expensive. *Keeps it anyway.* Your word is your word even when it costs you.

**"Who doesn't lend at interest to the desperate."** Doesn't profit off someone else's emergency. Doesn't turn a person's worst day into his payday.

**"And doesn't take a bribe against the innocent."** Can't be bought. Not for money, not for favor, not for access.

Ten marks. Every one of them about how you handle the people God put around you.

Then the promise — the last line:

**"Whoever does these things will never be shaken."**

*Never be shaken.* Not "will go to heaven someday." *Won't be knocked off his feet* — now, here, when the foundations shake (Psalm 11). The integrity isn't the entrance fee to God's tent. It's the *ballast*. It's what keeps you upright while everything else moves.

So — the guest list isn't about believing the right things. It's about whether the people in your life are safe with you.

Are they? Then you're already in the tent.

---

*Note on translation: v. 1 —* gur *= to sojourn, dwell as a welcomed stranger. v. 4 — "them that fear the LORD" =* yirei YHWH *=* yirah *= those who stand in awe/reverence — NOT those who are afraid. v. 5 — "usury" =* neshek *= interest charged to the poor (Exodus 22:25 forbids it against the needy specifically). "Moved" =* mot *= to totter, slip, be shaken.*
