# PSALM 83 — So They Will Know Your Name

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 83:1–18. The last Asaph psalm. Ten nations form a coalition to wipe Israel off the map. The prayer ends somewhere you don't expect.*

---

Ten enemies. One target. And a prayer that asks for their defeat — for a reason that'll surprise you.

**"God — don't stay silent. Don't be quiet, God. Don't be still."**

Three ways to say the same thing. *Silent. Quiet. Still.* Psalm 28 — *if You're silent, I'm as good as dead.* He's pounding.

**"See how Your enemies growl — how Your foes rear their heads."**

*Your* enemies. Asaph's move again. *This isn't about us. They're after YOU.*

**"With cunning they conspire against Your people. They plot against those You cherish. They say: 'Come — let us destroy them as a nation — so that Israel's name is remembered no more.'"**

*Remembered no more.* Not defeat. *Erasure.* They want the name gone. Not one person left to say *Israel*. That's the goal.

*Those You cherish.* The Hebrew is *tz'funecha* — Your hidden ones, Your treasured ones. The ones You keep in the safe. That's who they're after.

**"With one mind they plot together. They form an alliance against You — the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites — Moab and the Hagrites — Byblos, Ammon, Amalek — Philistia with the people of Tyre. Even Assyria has joined them — to strengthen the descendants of Lot."**

Ten names. Edom — Esau's kids. Ishmaelites — Abraham's other son. Moab and Ammon — Lot's kids. *Family.* Half this list is *relatives*. The people who should've been closest — lined up on the other side.

And *Assyria* — the superpower — backing them up.

*With one mind.* The Hebrew is *lev yachdav* — one heart, together. They agree on exactly one thing: *Israel has to go.* That's the only thing that unites them.

Now — Asaph goes to the record. Two battles from Judges:

**"Do to them as You did to Midian — as You did to Sisera and Jabin at the river Kishon — who perished at Endor and became like dung on the ground."**

*Midian* — Gideon, three hundred men with torches and jars, a whole army panicking in the dark (Judges 7). *Sisera and Jabin* — Deborah and Barak, chariots stuck in a flooded river, the general killed by a woman with a tent peg (Judges 4–5). Two wars Israel won without deserving to. *Do that again.*

**"Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb — all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna — who said: 'Let us take possession of the pasturelands of God.'"**

Four Midianite kings — all dead by Gideon's hand. *They said the same thing these ten are saying.*

Now — the imprecations. And they're vivid:

**"Make them like tumbleweed, my God — like chaff before the wind. As fire consumes the forest — as a flame sets the mountains ablaze — so pursue them with Your tempest. Terrify them with Your storm."**

*Tumbleweed.* Psalm 1's chaff. Rootless. Blown. *Fire on the mountains.* The whole hillside going up.

**"Cover their faces with shame —"**

Here it comes. Watch this. The reason for the whole prayer:

**"— so that they will seek Your name, Source."**

Stop.

*So that they will SEEK Your name.* Not "so they'll die." Not "so they'll suffer." *So they'll look for YOU.* Asaph asks for their shame — as a *doorway*. He wants the ten nations to lose so badly that they *turn around and ask Who beat them.*

That's a different prayer than it looked like. The tumbleweed, the fire, the storm — it's all *so that* they'll come looking for the name.

**"May they ever be ashamed and dismayed. May they perish in disgrace."**

Yes — still harsh. He's not soft. But he's already told you the *purpose*.

And the last verse — the target of everything:

**"Let them know that You — whose name is the Source — that You alone are the Most High over all the earth."**

*Let them KNOW.* The enemies. The relatives who turned. Edom, Moab, Ammon, Assyria. *Let them know Your name.* The psalm that started with *they want to erase OUR name* ends with *let them learn YOURS.*

That's the whole arc of Book Two and Three — the *nations*. Psalm 67's *so that*. Psalm 47's *the princes gather as Abraham's people*. Even the prayer against the coalition that wants Israel dead — ends with *let them know You.*

Eighteen verses. Here's the shape: *Don't be silent. Ten nations — half of them family — want our name erased. Do what You did to Midian. Make them tumbleweed. Fire on the mountain. Cover their faces with shame — SO THAT they'll seek Your name. Let them KNOW.*

When they come for your name: pray for theirs to be changed. Not by winning them over. By losing so badly they have to ask *Who did that?*
