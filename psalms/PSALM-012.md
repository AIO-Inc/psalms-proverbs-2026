# PSALM 12 — When Everyone Is Lying

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 12:1–8.*

---

Ever feel like you're the last honest person in the room?

"Help, Source — the loyal ones are gone. The faithful have vanished from among the children of Adam."

That's how it starts. Not "I have a problem." *There's nobody left I can trust.* Every day another person turns out to be working an angle.

"Everyone lies to his neighbor. They talk with smooth lips and a double heart."

*A double heart.* One heart for what they say, another for what they mean. The Hebrew literally says "a heart and a heart." Two of them. You never know which one you're talking to.

And David's prayer about it is blunt:

"Cut off the smooth lips, Source. Cut off the tongue that talks big — the ones who say, 'Our tongue is our power. Our mouth is our weapon. Who's going to be our master?'"

*Our tongue is our power.* Sound familiar? The people who've figured out that if you say something confidently enough, often enough, it becomes true — for enough people to get you what you want. Spin. Narrative. Talking points. They've made a god out of their own mouth.

Now here's where it turns, and this is one of the rare places in the Psalms where God Himself speaks:

**"'Because the poor are being plundered, because the needy are groaning — NOW I will rise up,' says the Source. 'I will put them in the safety they're longing for.'"**

*Now.* Not "someday." The groaning of the poor is the trigger. God hears people being crushed by the smooth-talkers and says *that's it, I'm getting up.*

And then David contrasts God's words with the liars' words:

**"The words of the Source are pure words — silver refined in a furnace, purified seven times."**

Seven times. Every drop of slag burned off. No angle, no spin, no double heart. When He says something, there's nothing hidden underneath it.

That's what you're starving for when everyone's lying. Not a louder voice. A *pure* one.

"You, Source, will guard them. You'll protect them from this generation forever."

*This generation.* He knows exactly which crowd he's talking about. And "forever" — the protection outlasts the liars.

Then the last line, and it's honest — he doesn't pretend the problem has gone away:

"The mark-missers are strutting around everywhere. What's worthless gets exalted among the children of Adam."

Still happening. Still true when he put the pen down. The liars are still strutting.

But he's said the other thing already. God's words are silver, seven times refined. God has said *now*. And you can build your whole life on one pure word from Him while a thousand double-hearted people talk.

Find the pure word. Stand on it. Let them strut.

---

*Note on translation: v. 2 —* b'lev va'lev *= literally "with a heart and a heart" — double-hearted, two-faced. v. 5 — direct divine speech, rare in the Psalter. v. 6 — the refining image: God's word has been through fire and has nothing hidden in it. "Godly" (v. 1) =* chasid *= loyal one. No* yirah*.*
