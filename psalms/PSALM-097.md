# PSALM 97 — Light Is Sown for the Righteous

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 97:1–12. Enthronement psalm — the King arrives in a storm, and the idols melt.*

---

Psalm 96 said *He comes*. Psalm 97 shows you what it looks like when He does.

**"The Source reigns! Let the earth be glad! Let the distant shores rejoice!"**

*The Source reigns.* Third time — 93, 96, now 97. *Distant shores* — the Hebrew is *iyim rabbim* — the many islands. The far edges. The places that can barely see the mainland. *They* rejoice too.

**"Clouds and thick darkness surround Him. Right-standing and justice are the foundation of His throne."**

*Clouds and darkness.* Sinai. Exodus 19 — the mountain wrapped in smoke. Psalm 18 — *He made darkness His covering*. The King comes in *weather*. You can't see Him directly. You see the *cloud*.

And *under* the cloud — the throne. And under the throne — *right-standing and justice*. Not power. Not force. The foundation is *fairness*. Psalm 89:14 said the same. The thing holding up the King's chair is *doing right*.

**"Fire goes before Him — and consumes His foes on every side."**

*Fire goes before.* Psalm 50 — *fire devours in front of Him*. The herald is *flame*. Everything that opposed Him — gone before He even arrives.

**"His lightning lights up the world. The earth sees and trembles."**

*The earth sees.* Not just people. The *planet* watches. Psalm 96 — *tremble before Him, all the earth*. Here it *does*.

**"The mountains melt like wax before the Source — before the Master of all the earth."**

*Melt like wax.* The most permanent thing in the landscape — *liquid*. Psalm 46 — *the mountains fall into the sea*. Psalm 68 — *wax melts before fire*. When the King shows up, the *geography* gives.

**"The heavens proclaim His right-standing — and all peoples see His glory."**

*All peoples see.* Not hear about. *See.* Psalm 98 will say the same. The vision of Book Four is *visible* — everyone on earth with their eyes on the same thing.

Now — the idols:

**"All who worship images are put to shame — those who boast in idols. Worship Him — all you gods!"**

*Worship Him — all you gods.* Psalm 96's *nothing-gods*. Psalm 82's failed rulers. Whatever they are — *they bow*. The King doesn't argue with rival gods. He *commands* them. And they kneel.

If there's something you've been bowing to — money, approval, the substance, the person — it's in this line. *Worship Him.* The command goes to the idol. It has to bow too.

**"Zion hears and rejoices. The villages of Judah are glad — because of Your judgments, Source."**

*Because of Your judgments.* They're *glad* about the judging. Because the judgments are *fair* — and fair judgment is good news for the people who've been getting the short end.

**"Because You, Source, are the Most High over all the earth. You are exalted far above all gods."**

Now — the turn from the storm to the *people*:

**"Let those who love the Source hate evil."**

*Love — hate.* Both. You can't love the King and be neutral about what He's against. Psalm 45 — *You've loved right-standing and hated wickedness*. If you love the one, you *hate* the other. Not the *people* — the *evil*. The thing that crushes them.

**"He guards the lives of His faithful ones — and delivers them from the hand of the wicked."**

*Guards. Delivers.* Psalm 91's shelter. Psalm 121's keeper. Same promise in the middle of the storm.

And then — the verse. The strangest and most beautiful image in the enthronement psalms:

**"Light is sown for the righteous — and joy for the upright in heart."**

*Light is SOWN.*

Read it again. *Sown.* Like seed. Like a farmer walking a field with a bag, scattering. But he's not scattering grain — he's scattering *light*. Into the dirt. Into the dark ground. Where you can't see it yet.

And what happens to seed? It goes *under*. It disappears. For a while, the field looks *empty*. Then — it comes *up*.

That's what God's doing for the righteous. *Sowing light.* You're standing in a dark field and it looks like nothing. But there's light *under the soil*. Planted. Germinating. And it's going to break the surface.

Psalm 88 — *darkness is my closest friend*. Psalm 97 — *light is sown for the righteous*. Same dark field. But the light's already in the ground.

*And joy.* Sown too. Coming up with it.

**"Rejoice in the Source, you who are righteous — and praise His holy name."**

*Rejoice.* Because the seed's in. You just can't see it yet.

Twelve verses. Here's the shape: *The Source reigns — even the islands rejoice. He comes in cloud and darkness — with justice under the throne. Fire ahead. Mountains melt. Everyone SEES. The idols bow — He commands them. Love Him, hate evil. He guards you. And — light is SOWN for you. It's in the ground. Rejoice.*

Your field looks dark? Check the soil. He's been planting.

---

*Note on translation: v. 1 —* iyim rabbim *= many islands/coastlands. v. 2 —* anan va-arafel *= cloud and thick darkness (Exod 19:9, 16; Deut 4:11). v. 5 —* ka-donag namasu *= like wax they melted. v. 7 —* hishtachavu-lo kol-elohim *= "bow down to Him, all gods." v. 10 —* ohavei YHWH sin'u ra *= "lovers of YHWH — hate evil." v. 11 —* or zaru'a la-tzadik *= "light [is] sown for the righteous" — some MSS read* zarach *(dawns/rises); the Masoretic* zaru'a *(sown) is retained and is the richer image. No* yirah*.*
