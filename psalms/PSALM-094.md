# PSALM 94 — Does He Who Made the Ear Not Hear?

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 94:1–23. The one lament in the middle of the enthronement psalms — because a real King has to deal with the people abusing power under Him.*

---

Psalm 93 said *the Source reigns*. Psalm 94 says — *then why are the wicked still running things?* It's the right question. And the psalm answers it.

**"Source — God who avenges — God who avenges — shine forth."**

*God who avenges.* Twice. The Hebrew is *El n'kamot* — God of vengeances. Not our vengeance. *His.* Romans 12 — *vengeance is Mine, says the Lord.* The psalmist isn't asking for permission to hit back. He's asking the *One who owns vengeance* to show up.

**"Rise up, Judge of the earth. Pay back to the proud what they deserve."**

**"How long, Source, will the wicked — how long will the wicked be jubilant?"**

*Jubilant.* Not just winning — *celebrating*. Throwing the party. Psalm 73's wicked with the fans. *How long* — the question again — but now specifically about the *gloating*.

**"They pour out arrogant words. All the evildoers are full of boasting. They crush Your people, Source. They oppress Your inheritance."**

*Your* people. *Your* inheritance. Asaph's move — make it God's problem.

**"They slay the widow and the foreigner. They murder the fatherless."**

The three groups every law in Torah protected — *widow, foreigner, orphan*. Exodus 22, Deuteronomy 10, 24, 27. The people with no one. And the wicked went straight for them. That's not random cruelty. That's *targeting*.

**"They say: 'The Source does not see. The God of Jacob pays no heed.'"**

Psalm 10, 14, 59, 64 — *who sees? who hears?* The oppressor's working assumption. *No one's watching.*

And now — the psalm turns and *argues* with them. And it's one of the sharpest arguments in the book:

**"Take notice, you senseless ones among the people. You fools — when will you become wise?"**

**"Does He who fashioned the ear not hear? Does He who formed the eye not see?"**

Stop and think about that.

*He MADE the ear.* Designed the eardrum, the tiny bones, the nerve that turns vibration into meaning. And you think He's *deaf*?

*He FORMED the eye.* The lens, the retina, the rods and cones, the whole miracle of light becoming sight. And you think He's *blind*?

The argument is: *the Maker of the sense has the sense.* You can't build a thing you don't understand. The One who invented hearing *hears*. The One who invented seeing *sees*. The oppressor's whole strategy — *nobody's watching* — collapses the moment you look at your own ear.

**"Does He who disciplines nations not punish? Does He who teaches mankind lack knowledge?"**

*He who teaches mankind.* Everything you know — He's the source of knowing. And you think He *doesn't know*?

**"The Source knows all human plans. He knows that they are futile."**

*Futile.* The Hebrew is *hevel* — vapor. Psalm 39, 62. Every scheme of the wicked — *breath*. He's seen the whole plan and knows how it ends before it starts.

Now — the turn toward the reader:

**"Happy is the one You discipline, Source — the one You teach from Your law."**

*Happy is the one You DISCIPLINE.* Read that again. The psalm's been about the wicked who get away with it. And it says — *you're the lucky one*. Because you're being *corrected*. They're not. Being disciplined means you're *in the family*. Hebrews 12 — *whom the Lord loves He disciplines*. The wicked's freedom from correction isn't a gift. It's *abandonment*.

**"You grant them relief from days of trouble — till a pit is dug for the wicked."**

*Till.* There's a *till*. Psalm 7's pit is being dug right now.

**"Because the Source will not reject His people. He will never forsake His inheritance."**

*Never.* The word Psalm 89 was afraid of. *Never forsake.*

**"Judgment will again be founded on right-standing — and all the upright in heart will follow it."**

*Again.* Justice *comes back*. The courts get fixed. Not in theory — in the psalm's certainty.

Now — the personal testimony:

**"Who will rise up for me against the wicked? Who will take a stand for me against evildoers? Unless the Source had given me help — I would soon have dwelt in the silence of death."**

*Unless.* One word between him and the grave. *Unless the Source had helped.* No other explanation for why he's still breathing.

**"When I said: 'My foot is slipping' — Your loyal-love, Source, supported me."**

*My foot is slipping.* Psalm 73 — *my feet almost stumbled*. Same edge. And — *loyal-love supported me*. He didn't catch himself. He was *caught*.

And then — the most tender verse in the psalm:

**"When anxiety was great within me — Your consolation brought me joy."**

*Anxiety.* The Hebrew is *sar'apai* — disquieting thoughts, the racing mind. The 3 a.m. loop. Psalm 77's insomnia.

*Your consolation.* The Hebrew is *tanchumecha* — Your *comforts*, plural. Not one big comfort. Many. Layered. The same root as Psalm 71's *nacham*.

*Brought me joy.* Not just quieted the anxiety. Turned it into *delight*. Psalm 30's *mourning into dancing*.

If you've got the racing mind — this verse. *When anxiety was great within me — Your comforts delighted me.*

**"Can a corrupt throne be allied with You — a throne that brings on misery by its decrees? The wicked band together against the righteous — and condemn the innocent to death."**

*A corrupt throne.* Psalm 82's gods. The judges who tilt the scales. *Allied with You?* No. God doesn't sit on that bench.

**"But the Source has become my fortress — and my God the rock in whom I take refuge."**

**"He will repay them for their sins — and destroy them for their wickedness. The Source our God will destroy them."**

*Our God.* Not "God in the abstract." *Ours.* The One who made the ear.

Twenty-three verses. Here's the shape: *God of vengeance — shine. How long will the wicked GLOAT? They murder the widow and the orphan and say no one sees. — He MADE the ear. He FORMED the eye. — Happy is the one He disciplines — that means you're His. He'll never forsake. My foot slipped — He caught me. My mind raced — His comforts delighted me. The corrupt throne isn't His. He's my fortress.*

When they say nobody's watching: look at your own ear. He built it. He's listening.
