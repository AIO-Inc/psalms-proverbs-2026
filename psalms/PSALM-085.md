# PSALM 85 — Loyal-Love and Truth Meet

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 85:1–13. Sons of Korah. After the return from exile — home, but not yet whole. And a vision of four virtues kissing.*

---

They're back. The exile's over. But the fields are bare and the walls are down and it doesn't feel like the promise yet. This is the prayer for the *gap* — between rescue and restoration.

**"You showed favor to Your land, Source. You restored the fortunes of Jacob. You forgave the wrongdoing of Your people. You covered all their sins. You set aside all Your wrath. You turned from Your fierce anger."**

Six things God *did*. Past tense. *Favor. Restored. Forgave. Covered. Set aside. Turned.* The singer starts with the record — because the record is true even when the present doesn't feel like it.

Then — the ask:

**"Restore us again, God our Savior. Put away Your displeasure toward us."**

*Again.* You did it before — do it *again*. Psalm 80's *hashivenu* — *turn us back*. The return from exile was one restoration. They need another.

**"Will You be angry with us forever? Will You prolong Your anger through all generations?"**

The *forever* question — even after coming home. Because coming home to rubble makes you wonder if the anger followed you.

**"Won't You revive us again — so Your people can rejoice in You?"**

*Revive.* The Hebrew is *t'chayenu* — make us *live*. Psalm 80:18 — *revive us and we'll call on Your name.* They're breathing. They're not *alive*.

**"Show us Your loyal-love, Source — and grant us Your salvation."**

Now — the singer stops talking and *listens*:

**"I will listen to what God the Source will say."**

That's a shift. Most of the psalm so far was *asking*. Now: *I'll shut up and hear.* Psalm 81 — the *unknown voice*. He's waiting for it.

**"He promises peace to His people — His loyal ones — but let them not turn to folly."**

*Peace.* *Shalom.* That's what he hears. Not "more waiting." *Peace.* And one condition: *don't go back to the stupidity that got you exiled.*

**"Surely His salvation is near to those who stand in awe of Him — that His glory may dwell in our land."**

*Near.* Psalm 75 — *Your name is near.* Salvation isn't far off. It's *close* — to the ones in *awe*. The wonder-keepers again. And the purpose: *that His glory may DWELL.* Not visit. *Live here.* In the land with the bare fields.

And then — the vision. Four virtues, personified, in a courtyard:

**"Loyal-love and truth meet together. Right-standing and peace kiss each other."**

Stop and see it.

*Chesed* — loyal-love — walking in from one direction. *Emet* — truth — from the other. They *meet*. Not collide. *Meet.* Like old friends in a marketplace.

*Tzedek* — right-standing, justice — and *shalom* — peace. They *kiss*.

Why is that astonishing? Because in the world, they *don't*. Love and truth pull apart — you either tell someone the truth or you protect their feelings. Justice and peace fight — you either punish the guilty or keep the peace. Every human system picks two and loses two.

And the psalm says: *in God's land — they kiss.* All four. Nobody has to lose. The truth gets told *and* the love holds. Justice is done *and* peace stays. That's not a compromise. That's a *miracle*. Augustine, Luther, the church fathers — they all read this as the cross. The one place where God's justice was fully done *and* His mercy fully given *in the same act*.

**"Truth springs up from the earth. Right-standing looks down from heaven."**

*Truth from the ground. Justice from the sky.* Both directions at once. Earth reaching up. Heaven reaching down. They meet in the middle — where people live.

**"The Source will surely give what is good. Our land will yield its harvest."**

*The land will yield.* The bare fields — full. Psalm 65's harvest, promised to the people standing in the empty ones.

**"Right-standing goes before Him — and prepares the way for His steps."**

*Prepares the way.* Isaiah 40 — *prepare the way of the Lord.* John the Baptist. Justice walks ahead of God like a herald — clearing the road. When you see right being done — He's coming behind it.

Thirteen verses. Here's the shape: *You did it before — six things. Do it again. How long? Revive us. — I'll listen. — He says peace. Salvation is near to the ones in awe. Loyal-love and truth meet. Justice and peace kiss. Truth from the ground, justice from the sky. The land will yield. He's coming down the road justice cleared.*

You're home but it's rubble? Listen. Four virtues are kissing in the courtyard. He's on the road behind them.
