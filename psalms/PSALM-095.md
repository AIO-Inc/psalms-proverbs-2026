# PSALM 95 — Today, If You Hear His Voice

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 95:1–11. A call to worship that turns into a warning. Hebrews 3–4 built an entire argument on it. The key word is "today."*

---

Half invitation. Half alarm. And the hinge is one word.

**"Come — let us sing for joy to the Source! Let us shout aloud to the Rock of our salvation!"**

*Come.* Plural. *Let us.* This isn't a private psalm. It's a *summons* — somebody standing at the temple gate calling the crowd in.

**"Let us come before Him with thanksgiving — and extol Him with music and song."**

*Thanksgiving first.* Psalm 50 — *offer God thanksgiving*. Before you ask for anything — thank.

**"Because the Source is the great God — the great King above all gods."**

*Above all gods.* Psalm 82's gods — the powers, the rulers, the things people bow to. *Above* them all. The enthronement theme continues.

**"In His hand are the depths of the earth — and the mountain peaks belong to Him. The sea is His — He made it — and His hands formed the dry land."**

*Depths and peaks.* The lowest and the highest — *in His hand*. *The sea* — the chaos — *His. He made it.* Psalm 93's roaring waves — *He built them*. You don't fear what you manufactured.

Now — the posture changes. From shouting to kneeling:

**"Come — let us bow down in worship. Let us kneel before the Source our Maker."**

*Bow. Kneel.* The body goes down. After the shout — the *silence*. Both are worship. Psalm 47 said clap. Psalm 95 says kneel. You need both.

*Our Maker.* Not just the world's — *ours*. He formed the dry land and He formed *you*.

**"Because He is our God — and we are the people of His pasture — the flock under His care."**

*The flock under His care.* Psalm 23, 74, 79, 80 — *sheep*. Always sheep. Because sheep don't manage themselves.

And then — the hinge. The word Hebrews built two chapters on:

**"Today — if only you would hear His voice —"**

*Today.* *Ha-yom.* Not tomorrow. Not "when I've got things sorted." *Today.*

*If only you would hear.* Not "if He would speak." He's *speaking*. The question is whether you'll *hear*. Psalm 81 — *if only My people would listen*. The voice isn't the problem. The ear is.

Hebrews 3:7–4:11 quotes this and says: *the "today" is still open. As long as it's called "today" — you can still go in.* The door didn't close with Israel. It's *now*.

Now — the warning. God speaking:

**"'— do not harden your hearts as you did at Meribah — as you did that day at Massah in the wilderness — where your ancestors tested Me. They tried Me — though they had seen what I did.'"**

*Meribah. Massah.* Exodus 17, Numbers 20. The places Israel demanded water and said *is the Source among us or not?* — *after* the sea split, *after* the manna fell. They'd *seen it*. And they still asked *is He even here?*

*Harden your hearts.* The Hebrew is *al-takshu* — don't make it *stiff*. The heart that's seen the miracle and still says *prove it* — that's not doubt. That's *hardness*. Doubt asks. Hardness *demands*.

**"'For forty years I was angry with that generation. I said: They are a people whose hearts go astray — and they have not known My ways.'"**

*Forty years.* An entire generation — the ones who came out of Egypt — wandered until they died. Psalm 90's Moses watched every funeral. *Their hearts went astray* — not their feet. The feet followed the cloud. The *hearts* wandered.

**"'So I declared on oath in My anger: They shall never enter My rest.'"**

*My rest.* The land. Canaan. The promise. They got *close* — Moses saw it from the mountain — and *didn't go in*. Not because God changed His mind. Because they *wouldn't trust Him for the next thing* after He'd done the last thing.

And here's Hebrews' point: *there's still a rest. Today. The door's open. Don't be the generation that saw everything and went in for nothing.*

Eleven verses. Here's the shape: *Come — shout. He's above all gods. He built the sea. Come — kneel. We're His sheep. TODAY — if you'd hear His voice — don't stiffen. They saw the miracles and still said "prove it." Forty years. They never entered. — You still can.*

*Today* is the only day you've ever been given. Hear the voice in it. Go in.
