# PSALM 115 — Those Who Make Them Will Be Like Them

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 115:1–18. Third Hallel psalm — sung after the Passover meal. About idols — what they can't do, and what happens to the people who trust them.*

---

The meal's over. The cups are refilled. And the family sings about *idols*. Because Passover is the night you remember which God actually showed up.

**"Not to us, Source — not to us — but to Your name be the glory — because of Your loyal-love and faithfulness."**

*Not to us.* Twice. The night they remember being *rescued* — and the first thing they say is *we didn't do it*. No credit taken. *Your name.*

**"Why do the nations say: 'Where is their God?'"**

Psalm 42, 79 — the taunt. *Where is He?* And the answer:

**"Our God is in heaven. He does whatever pleases Him."**

*Whatever pleases Him.* Not "whatever we ask." Not "whatever the nations expect." *Whatever He pleases.* That's the answer to *where is He* — He's *free*. Not on a leash. Not in a box. The nations want to see Him because they think gods live in boxes. Theirs do.

Now — the idol section. And it's *funny*. Deliberately:

**"But their idols are silver and gold — made by human hands."**

*Made by human hands.* That's the whole problem in five words. You *made* it. How's it going to save you?

**"They have mouths — but cannot speak. Eyes — but cannot see. They have ears — but cannot hear. Noses — but cannot smell. They have hands — but cannot feel. Feet — but cannot walk. Nor can they utter a sound with their throats."**

Seven body parts. Seven failures. *Mouth — silent. Eyes — blind. Ears — deaf. Nose — no smell. Hands — numb. Feet — stuck. Throat — nothing.* The idol's got *everything* a god should have — and *none* of it works. It's a mannequin. A very expensive mannequin.

Psalm 94 — *He who made the ear — does He not hear?* Psalm 115 — *they made an ear — and it can't.* God builds ears that work. Humans build ears that don't.

And then — the verse. The one that should scare you a little:

**"Those who make them will be like them — and so will all who trust in them."**

*Like them.*

You become what you worship. That's the law. Trust the silent thing — you go silent. Trust the blind thing — you stop seeing. Trust the thing with hands that can't feel — your hands go numb. The idol doesn't just *fail* you. It *shapes* you. Into itself.

Psalm 112 said the opposite: *the one in awe of God becomes gracious and compassionate — God's words.* Psalm 115: *the one who trusts the idol becomes deaf, blind, and mute — the idol's words.* Same principle. Different direction. You *always* turn into what you're looking at.

What's your idol? The thing you trust that has no ears? The phone. The number in the account. The approval. Look at it hard — *does it hear you?* Then look at what it's doing to your *hearing*.

Now — the turn. Three groups, one command:

**"All you Israelites — trust in the Source. He is their help and shield. House of Aaron — trust in the Source. He is their help and shield. You who stand in awe of Him — trust in the Source. He is their help and shield."**

*Israel. Aaron. Those in awe.* The people, the priests, and — *the ones in awe*. That third group isn't ethnic. It's not a bloodline. It's *anyone amazed by Him*. Psalm 87 — *born in Zion*. The circle widens to *whoever's gasping*.

*Help and shield.* Three times. The idol has hands that can't feel. God is *help and shield* — hands that *work*.

**"The Source remembers us and will bless us. He will bless His people Israel. He will bless the house of Aaron. He will bless those who stand in awe of Him — small and great alike."**

*Remembers.* Psalm 105, 106 — He *remembers*. *Small and great alike.* No tier system. The kid and the king — same blessing.

**"May the Source cause you to flourish — both you and your children."**

*Your children.* Psalm 78, 103, 112. Always the kids.

**"Praise be to the Source — the Maker of heaven and earth."**

*Maker.* Not made. The idols were *made by hands*. The Source *made the hands*.

**"The highest heavens belong to the Source — but the earth He has given to mankind."**

*Given to mankind.* Psalm 8 — *You made him ruler over the works of Your hands*. The earth's *yours*. Steward it. Don't build gods out of it.

And then — the reason to praise *now*:

**"It is not the dead who praise the Source — those who go down to the place of silence. It is we who extol the Source — both now and forevermore."**

*Not the dead.* Psalm 6, 30, 88 — *can dust praise You?* The Hallel's answer: *no — so praise while you're breathing.* The idol has a throat that can't make a sound. The dead have the same problem. *You* don't. Yet. Use it.

**"Praise the Source!"**

Eighteen verses. Here's the shape: *Not to us — to Your name. Where's our God? In heaven, doing what He pleases. Their idols — mouth, eyes, ears, nose, hands, feet, throat — all broken. And you become what you trust. — Israel, Aaron, everyone in awe — trust the Source. He's help and shield. He blesses small and great. He made heaven and earth. The dead can't praise — so praise NOW.*

Jesus sang this after the last meal. *Those who trust idols become like them.* Then He walked out to a garden — and became like *us*. Dust. Death. So we could become like *Him*.

Look at what you're trusting. Check if it has ears. Then turn around.
