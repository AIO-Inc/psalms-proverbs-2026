# PSALM 128 — Your Children Like Olive Shoots

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 128:1–6. Ninth Song of Ascent. Six verses. The companion to 127 — the house God builds, seen from the dinner table. Two yirahs. Both awe.*

---

Psalm 127 gave you the *arrows*. Psalm 128 shows you the *table* they're sitting at.

**"Happy are all who stand in awe of the Source — who walk in obedience to Him."**

*Ashrei.* Psalm 1, 112, 119. *Happy.* And — *who stand in awe*. *Yirah.* The 111:10 person. The one who's *amazed*, not afraid. Psalm 112 described that person's *character*. Psalm 128 describes their *kitchen*.

**"You will eat the fruit of your labor. Blessings and prosperity will be yours."**

*Eat the fruit of your labor.* Psalm 127 — *in vain you rise early*. Psalm 128 — *you'll actually EAT what you worked for*. That's the difference between building without Him and *with* Him. Same labor. One's vapor. One's *dinner*.

The curse in Deuteronomy 28 was *you'll plant and someone else will eat it*. The blessing here is *you'll eat what you planted*. Simple. Not "you'll be rich." *Your work will feed you.*

Now — the table:

**"Your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house."**

*A fruitful vine.* Psalm 80 — *You brought a vine out of Egypt.* Israel was the vine. Here — the *wife* is. *Fruitful* — the Hebrew is *poriyah* — bearing, producing. And — *within your house*. Not in the field. *Inside.* The vine that grows up the walls of the home and makes it *alive*.

In the ancient world, a vine on the house was the sign of *peace*. Nobody plants a vine when they're about to flee. A vine takes *years*. It means *we're staying*.

**"Your children will be like olive shoots around your table."**

*Olive shoots.* Psalm 52 — *I'm like a green olive tree in the house of God.* Psalm 92 — *still bearing fruit in old age.* And now — the *shoots*. The young ones. Around the table.

Here's what olive shoots do: they come up *from the root* of the old tree. Around its base. Same root — new trunks. A thousand-year-old olive tree is *surrounded* by its own children, growing from its own base. That's the image. The kids aren't *separate* plants. They're *the same tree — coming up again*. Psalm 71's transmission — *until I declare to the next generation*. Psalm 128 — *and there they are, around the table, from the same root.*

**"Yes — this will be the blessing for the man who stands in awe of the Source."**

Second *yirah*. And notice what the blessing *is*. Not a palace. Not a kingdom. A *table*. A wife who's a vine. Kids who are shoots. *Dinner.* The person in *awe of God* gets — *a full table*.

Because the person who's *afraid of God* can't sit still long enough to eat. And the person who *ignores* God rises early and stays up late — Psalm 127 — and never gets to the table. The *awe*-person *sits down*.

**"May the Source bless you from Zion — all the days of your life. May you see the prosperity of Jerusalem."**

*From Zion.* The pilgrims are *there* — Psalm 122, 125. The blessing comes from the hill they're standing on. And — *the prosperity of Jerusalem* — Psalm 122's *pray for the peace of the city*. The home table and the holy city — blessed *together*. Psalm 51 — David's heart and Jerusalem's walls. The private and the public — one blessing.

**"May you live to see your children's children."**

*Children's children.* Psalm 78, 103. The olive shoots — *having shoots*. The old tree with two generations growing from its base. That's the long game. Not "may you be successful." *May you see the grandkids.*

**"Peace be on Israel."**

*Shalom al-Yisrael.* Psalm 125's last line. Same words. The Ascents keep ending in *shalom*.

Six verses. Here's the shape: *Happy is the one in AWE. You'll eat what you worked for. Your wife's a vine on the house — nobody plants a vine when they're leaving. Your kids are olive shoots — same root, new trunks, around the table. That's the blessing for the awe-person. From Zion. See the grandkids. Peace on Israel.*

Here's what to take: the blessing in this psalm is *dinner*. A table. People around it. The one who's amazed by God gets to *sit down and eat*. That's the payoff. Not because the awe-person earned a feast. Because *awe* is the thing that lets you *stop working and sit*.

Pull up a chair. The shoots are around the table.
