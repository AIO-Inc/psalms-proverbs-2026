# PSALM 33 — He Spoke, and It Was

*Motivational script in today's vernacular. Source: Hebrew text, Psalm 33:1–22. Twenty-two verses — one for every letter of the Hebrew alphabet. A hymn to the God who made everything by talking.*

---

"Shout for joy in the Source, you who do right! Praise fits the upright."

*Fits.* Like a good jacket. Praise isn't a duty for the upright — it's the thing that *looks right* on them.

"Thank the Source with the harp. Make music with the ten-string lyre. **Sing Him a new song.** Play skillfully, with a shout."

*A new song.* Not the same one your grandparents sang — not *only* that. Something you wrote this week, about what He did this week. He wants fresh material.

*Skillfully.* Practice. Get good. He's worth the effort.

"Because the word of the Source is straight. All His work is done in faithfulness. He loves right-standing and justice. **The earth is full of the loyal-love of the Source.**"

*Full.* Not sprinkled. *Full.* Wherever you're standing, you're standing in it.

Now the creation section — and this is what Psalm 33 is famous for:

**"By the word of the Source the heavens were made. By the breath of His mouth — all their host. He gathers the waters of the sea like a heap. He puts the deep in storehouses."**

No hammer. No fight with a chaos monster like the other ancient stories. He *talked*. He breathed out and there were stars. Every galaxy is an exhale.

And the sea — the thing the ancients were most afraid of — He *stacks it up like grain* and puts it in a warehouse. The terror of the world is inventory to Him.

**"Let all the earth stand in awe of the Source. Let everyone who lives in the world be amazed at Him. Because He spoke — and it was. He commanded — and it stood."**

*He spoke — and it was.* Five words in Hebrew. *Hu amar va-yehi.* That's how everything started. He said it and it happened. If that's who you're talking to when you pray — what are you worried about?

Now — the powers of this world:

"The Source breaks the plan of the nations. He frustrates the schemes of the peoples. **The plan of the Source stands forever. The thoughts of His heart — to all generations.**"

Two kinds of plans. Theirs — broken. His — forever. Every empire had a plan. Where are they? He's still working the same plan He had at the beginning, and it's on schedule.

**"Happy is the nation whose God is the Source — the people He's chosen for His inheritance."**

Then — the eye. Three times:

"The Source looks down from heaven. He *sees* all the children of Adam. From where He sits He *watches* everyone who lives on earth — **He who forms all their hearts, who understands all their works.**"

He *formed* your heart. He knows how it's wired because He did the wiring. When you can't understand your own reactions — He can. He's got the schematic.

Now the horses again — same point as Psalm 20:

"A king isn't saved by his big army. A warrior isn't rescued by his great strength. **A horse is a false hope for safety.** With all its power it can't rescue anyone."

*A false hope.* The Hebrew is *sheker* — a lie. The horse is a *lie* you tell yourself about what keeps you safe. Your version might be the 401k, the degree, the connections, the plan B. Fine things. Lying to you.

**"Look — the eye of the Source is on those who stand in awe of Him, on those who wait for His loyal-love — to rescue their soul from death, to keep them alive in famine."**

The eye that sees everyone — it *rests* on the ones in awe. Not because they're better. Because they're *looking back*. Eye contact.

"Our soul waits for the Source. He's our help and our shield. Our heart celebrates in Him — because we've trusted His holy name."

And the last line — the prayer the whole hymn was building toward:

**"Let Your loyal-love be on us, Source — just as we've waited for You."**

*Just as we've waited.* Match it. We've been standing here looking up. Meet us at the same intensity.

The One who spoke galaxies into being knows your heart's wiring, has His eye on you, and calls your backup plan a lie. Sing Him a new song. Then wait — and He'll match you.
